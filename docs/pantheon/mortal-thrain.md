# Overview

- **Domains**: Community, Craft, Dwarf, Family

## Appearance

Thrain is a stout and dignified dwarf, adorned in robes of earthy tones that mirror the colors of the mountain range he calls home. His beard, intricately braided with gemstones, radiates a calming aura. Thrain's eyes, though filled with the wisdom of ages, convey a deep serenity. He is often depicted carrying a staff that doubles as a mighty warhammer, symbolizing the balance between strength and peace.

## Backstory

Thrain Stoneheart emerged from the heart of the Old Spine Mountains, a region known for its stoic dwarven clans and the enduring strength of its peaks. From a young age, Thrain exhibited a unique understanding of balance and harmony. Rather than focusing solely on the traditional dwarven pursuits of mining and warfare, Thrain sought a path that combined strength with tranquility.

As he delved into the ancient dwarven arts of craftsmanship and forging, Thrain discovered a profound connection between the act of creation and the fostering of peace. His creations, imbued with both physical and spiritual strength, became symbols of unity among the dwarven clans.

## Divine Realm

Thrain's divine realm, known as the Hearthforge, is a vast cavern within the Old Spine Mountains. Bathed in the warm glow of eternal hearthfires, this realm serves as a meeting place for beings of all races and creeds who seek resolution and understanding. The rhythmic echoes of hammer strikes and the harmonious hum of communal work resonate through the cavern, reflecting the forging of alliances and the pursuit of shared prosperity.

## Tenets of Thrain

- **Balance in All Things**: Thrain encourages his followers to seek harmony in their actions, understanding that both strength and peace are essential components of a fulfilling life.

- **The Forge of Unity**: Followers are taught to use their skills and talents to build bridges between communities and bring people together. The act of creation, whether through art, craftsmanship, or diplomacy, is seen as a sacred pursuit.

- **Defender of the Peaceful**: Thrain's worshipers are tasked with defending the innocent and protecting those who cannot protect themselves. However, this defense is not only physical but extends to the preservation of cultural heritage, traditions, and the pursuit of knowledge.

## Holy Symbol

The symbol of Thrain Stoneheart is a stylized anvil, with a hammer crossed over it. This represents the forging of strength and peace, emphasizing the balance that the god encourages his followers to maintain.

## Clergy and Temples

The clergy of Thrain consists of both skilled artisans and wise diplomats. Temples dedicated to the Tranquil Forgemaster are often carved into the heart of mountains, their halls adorned with masterfully crafted sculptures and tapestries that depict tales of unity and cooperation.

## Rivalries

While Thrain seeks peaceful resolution, he holds an enduring disdain for deities who thrive on chaos and discord. His teachings position him in opposition to those who revel in war for its own sake, and he actively opposes those who seek to disrupt the delicate balance of peace.
