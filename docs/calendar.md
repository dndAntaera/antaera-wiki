# Overview

The lunar calendar of Antæra, a sophisticated timekeeping system bestowed by Selene, believed to be an ancient lunar scholar, holds profound significance across the diverse cultures of Antæra. This meticulously structured calendar divides the year into 360 days, organized into twelve months, each consisting of thirty days. This division is meticulously aligned with the lunar cycles, where each phase—New Moon, Waxing Moon, Full Moon, and Waning Moon—plays a critical role in shaping agricultural practices, spiritual rituals, and communal life.

One of the most intriguing aspects of this calendar is the variation in the naming of the months across different Antæran cultures. Reflecting the region's rich linguistic diversity, each culture uses its unique terminology for the lunar phases to name the months. This not only shows the calendar's deep integration into daily life but also highlights its role as a significant cultural marker. For instance, while one culture might refer to the full moon month as a time of brightness and completion, another might have a name that emphasizes the spiritual or communal gatherings typical of this phase. This variability underscores how the lunar calendar is woven into the very fabric of each society, influencing not just timekeeping but also cultural identity and practices.

The lunar calendar’s impact extends significantly beyond mere scheduling. It dictates the timing of key cultural festivals, religious observances, and legal proceedings, ensuring that such activities coincide with specific lunar phases believed to enhance their effectiveness or significance. For example, the New Moon is often associated with ceremonies of renewal and initiation—ideal for starting new endeavors or planting crops. Conversely, the Full Moon is traditionally a time for gathering and celebrating community achievements, often accompanied by festivals that highlight social unity and spiritual fulfillment.

Additionally, the appearance of blood moons in the calendar marks significant celestial events that herald the absence and return of the moon. These blood moons are not only astronomical phenomena but also carry deep cultural and spiritual significance. They are often seen as portents of change or moments of powerful transformation, and are incorporated into myths and legends, influencing cultural narratives and practices.

The influence of the lunar calendar is also evident in the daily rhythms of Antæran society. Agricultural practices, particularly, are closely aligned with the phases of the moon. Planting, nurturing, and harvesting crops are timed to correspond with specific lunar conditions believed to optimize growth and yield. Similarly, the timing of legal and political events is strategically planned to align with lunar phases that are seen as auspicious or symbolically appropriate, such as making declarations during the New Moon or signing treaties during the Full Moon.

# The Seasons
**Spring**

- Yestë Nulorë (New Moon): Introduces spring with new beginnings and an increase in benevolent fey activity, aiding the natural reawakening.
- Silloros (Waxing Crescent): Supports visible growth and rejuvenation, enhanced by the nurturing magic of the fey.
- Erandur (First Quarter Moon): Balances growth with practical action, utilizing the fey's presence to ensure ecological stability.
**Summer**

- Aurindil (Waxing Gibbous): Brings a period of vigorous growth and high activity, with extended daylight allowing communities to thrive.
- Pelnorë (Full Moon): Celebrates the peak of summer with widespread festivals under the full moon, emphasizing joy and communal unity.
- Calindil (Waning Gibbous): Focuses on harvesting and sharing the season's bounty, preparing for the less bountiful months ahead.
**Autumn**

- Telcandur (Last Quarter Moon): Introduces autumn with a deeper presence of unseelie fey, adding unpredictability to the natural decline.
- Loroscal (Waning Crescent): Prepares for winter's return with increased caution due to the heightened activity of unseelie fey, focusing on conservation and introspection.
**Winter**

- Loroscal Súrë (Waning Crescent Blood Moon): Marks the onset of winter with intense darkness and heightened danger as the blood moon intensifies nocturnal threats.
- Indormë Yestë (Hidden Moon, beginning): Continues the deep darkness, exacerbating peril with almost pitch-black nights and increased monster activity.
- Indormë Telumë (Hidden Moon, end): The year starts with strategic planning and strengthening communal bonds to withstand the darkness.
- Nulorë Súrë (New Blood Moon): Concludes winter with transformative energy, signaling a shift towards renewal and decreased nocturnal dangers.

# Months

1. **Indormë Telumë (Hidden Moon, ending)**: The new year continues under the cloak of darkness with Indormë Telumë, fostering an atmosphere of introspection and communal planning. This period is crucial for setting goals and strengthening defenses in preparation for the challenges ahead.
1. **Nulorë Súrë (New Blood Moon)**: Winter concludes with the intense energy of Nulorë Súrë, a blood moon signaling the eventual return of light. This month brings a mix of challenge and renewal, setting the stage for the reawakening of the world with the onset of spring.
1. **Yestë Nulorë (New Moon)**: Spring is ushered in by Yestë Nulorë, which is marked by an increase in fey activity, particularly from the more benevolent and playful fey. This magical influence aids in the natural reawakening, contributing to the enchantment and renewal of the world.
1. **Silloros (Waxing Crescent)**: The waxing crescent of Silloros supports visible growth and rejuvenation, enhanced by the mystical fey who nurture emerging life. This period is characterized by magical growth and a flourishing of both plant and animal life, imbued with fey magic.
1. **Erandur (First Quarter Moon)**: Erandur balances the growth of spring with practical action, underpinned by a mystical balance maintained by the fey. Their presence ensures ecological stability, allowing life to thrive and communities to prepare for the abundant months ahead.
1. **Aurindil (Waxing Gibbous)**: The lively month of Aurindil brings extended daylight and diminished fey activity, focusing on the peak activities of summer. Communities engage in preparations for the upcoming celebrations and communal gatherings.
1. **Pelnorë (Full Moon)**: Pelnorë celebrates the zenith of summer with widespread community festivals under the full moon, featuring feasting, music, and dance. These gatherings honor the abundance and vibrancy of the season, fostering strong communal bonds.
1. **Calindil (Waning Gibbous)**: As the moon wanes, Calindil marks the beginning of the harvest season. Communities share their bounty and knowledge, with festivals celebrating the harvest and the generosity of the land.
1. **Telcandur (Last Quarter Moon)**: Autumn deepens with Telcandur, where the waning moon calls for introspection and the completion of cycles. This month is about tying up loose ends, finalizing projects, and settling conflicts, ensuring a smooth transition to the quieter times ahead.
1. **Loroscal (Waning Crescent)**: With the further waning of the moon, Loroscal continues autumn's preparation for winter, underscored by the increasing activity of unseelie fey. Their presence heightens the need for caution, as their mischievous and sometimes malevolent behaviors influence the season’s end.
1. **Loroscal Súrë (Waning Crescent Blood Moon)**: Winter begins under the ominous glow of Loroscal Súrë. The blood moon heightens the darkness and emboldens malevolent creatures, increasing the danger. Communities fortify their defenses, employing protective rituals to survive the peak of nocturnal threats.
1. **Indormë Yestë (Hidden Moon, beginning)**: The year ends in deep darkness with Indormë Yestë, exacerbating the peril with almost pitch-black nights and heightened monster activity. This intense darkness requires strong communal resilience and continuous vigilance.

# Days

1. **Solenday**: The dawn of the week, Solenday, is deeply intertwined with the rising of the sun and new beginnings, embodying the optimism and potential that a new week holds. This day is celebrated as a symbol of renewal, where the rising sun heralds the promise of fresh starts and new opportunities. Communities might engage in rituals of cleansing or planning sessions for the week ahead, embracing the rejuvenating energy that Solenday represents. A common misconception persists that this day is named after the god Sol.
1. **Lunaday**: Lunaday is steeped in the mystical influence of the moon, a day when its presence is felt most strongly across the lands. With a higher frequency of full moons occurring on this day, it is a time for reflection, fertility, and the celebration of the moon's guiding light. People might gather to observe lunar rituals, reflect on their inner selves, and seek the moon's guidance for the coming days.
1. **Tidestar**: Once dedicated to a legendary sailor, Tidestar has evolved into a practical day for economic affairs, particularly among tradespeople who settle their dues and plan their financial strategies. It's a day marked by the bustling activity of markets and ports, as communities come together to manage the lifeblood of trade and commerce that sustains them. This day is also colloquially known as "market day" and is the day on which most markets have sales and some merchants get new stock.
1. **Earthrise**: Originating as a signal for farmers to commence their crop rotations, Earthrise now symbolizes a broader call to initiate new endeavors. It's a day when people of all professions are encouraged to plant the seeds of future projects, both literally and metaphorically. Earthrise embodies growth, nurturing, and the anticipation of what hard work and care might bring to fruition.
1. **Wildsday**: Named after the enigmatic emergence of the Sidhe Wilderness, Wildsday is a day when the veil between the Prime Material Plane and the fey realms thins, leading to an increase in fey activity and whimsy. It's a time of magic and unpredictability, celebrated with festivals that honor the fey and the mysterious beauty of the wilds that surround the communities.
1. **Stormkeep**: What started as a sailors' tradition now encompasses all adventurers setting forth on their quests. Stormkeep is a day of departure, imbued with the spirit of adventure and the anticipation of journeys ahead. Many pray to [Vaylen](deity/vaylen.md) for a blessing on this day, hoping for safe passage and perhaps a touch of divine favor as they embark on their travels. It's a day marked by farewells, preparations, and the excitement of the unknown.
1. **Etherend**: Named in honor of a legendary mage, Etherend is a day dedicated to the pursuit of knowledge and the arcane. Scholars, mages, and students alike use this day to organize their studies, embark on new academic endeavors, and delve deeper into the mysteries of magic. It's a day of quiet study and reflection, punctuated by the sharing of insights and discoveries among the learned communities.

# Eras

## 1E: The Mythical Era
Marked by the arrival of the Eladrin on the Prime Material Plane in the region later named the Mir'raask Gulf, this epoch heralds the inception of what is known today by archaeologists as "Ancient Antæra." These trailblazers, the "Ancient Antærans," engineered a civilization of profound influence, shrouded in the mysteries of its origin. The reasons for their departure from the Sidhe Wilderness are veiled in obscurity, with no surviving records to elucidate this monumental exodus. Fragments of ancient texts, scarce yet invaluable, offer glimpses into their migration and the indelible mark it left on the world. These texts, coupled with the awe-inspiring architectural relics scattered across the globe, testify to the Ancient Antærans' sophisticated mastery of construction and magic.

As enigmatic as its beginning, the conclusion of the Mythical Era is defined by the sudden, unexplained disappearance of the Ancient Antærans. Their advanced society, known for its widespread architectural wonders, mysteriously faded, leaving no trace of their whereabouts. This baffling end to the Ancient Antærans' chapter has remained a source of intrigue and speculation among scholars. The enduring remnants of their constructions stand as silent witnesses to a bygone age of discovery, innovation, and enigma.

It is currently believed by scholars that Vortressa, who is now recognized as the goddess of monsters, was originally an ambitious mage during the Mythical Era. Her quest for power led her to explore the darker side  of summoning, through which she introduced a myriad of monsters and other formidable creatures from distant realities into Antæra. These creatures, with their varied and terrifying forms, became a significant part of Antæra’s ecosystem, shaping its landscapes and legends. As these monstrous beings spread across the continent, their unwavering loyalty and worship towards Vortressa provided her with a unique source of divine power. This worship by her monstrous creations eventually elevated her to godhood, establishing Vortressa as a deity who is linked to the very horrors she summoned into the world.

## 2E: The Era of Emergences
In the initial dawn of the Era of Emergences, the world was graced with the divine creation of the smallfolk races by Orion. Gnomes emerged from the radiance of Planar Crystals, each variant reflecting the stone's unique magic. From the world's deepest ores, the dwarves were shaped, as enduring and valuable as the metals from which they were formed. Halflings, born from the earth's welcoming clay, embodied adaptability and a profound connection to life. This burst of creation infused the world with diversity and potential, marking a vibrant beginning for these burgeoning societies.

However, this diversity also sowed seeds of division among the gnomes, leading to the Wars of Crystal Discord. This era of strife showcased the gnomes' ingenuity but also highlighted their internal conflicts, affecting the broader tapestry of smallfolk communities. In response to the turmoil, dwarves sought refuge in the earth's depths, crafting splendid subterranean kingdoms, while halflings found kinship and peace within elven societies, demonstrating their inherent adaptability.

The conclusion of the Era of Emergences was heralded by the elves' intervention to quell the gnome conflicts. Their wise and decisive actions brought an end to the discord, paving the way for a period of healing and reconciliation. This pivotal moment, facilitated by the elves, not only ended the hostilities but also marked the beginning of a new chapter focused on rebuilding and fostering unity among the races.

The Era of Emergences, with its remarkable beginnings, challenging conflicts, and transformative resolutions, laid the foundations for a world rich in culture and communal harmony. The elves' peacemaking efforts underscored the era's end, ushering in a legacy of cooperation and understanding that would influence the development of interconnected societies, celebrating the diversity and resilience of the world's inhabitants.

The Era of Emergences spanned approximately five centuries, a testament to the enduring impact of its events and the lasting legacy of the races it brought forth. This period of time, though but a chapter in the world's expansive history, remains a critical juncture that shaped the societal, cultural, and political landscapes that followed.

## 3E: The Era of Man
Spanning approximately 1000 years, the Era of Man was initiated by a monumental exodus led by Ukrol, who guided humanity from a distant reality into Antæra, settling the fertile Antæran Plains. Under Ukrol’s divine directive, humans quickly established an expansive, imperialistic empire with the intention of dominating the continent’s native races. This era of human ascendancy, marked by their swift development and expansion, posed a unique terror to the longer-lived races such as elves and halflings. The rapid pace at which humans could learn, adapt, and grow, compounded by their shorter life spans, made their empire’s spread both relentless and devastating.

The conflict that ensued engulfed the continent, as the human empire sought to subjugate or exterminate those who opposed their dominion. This dark chapter saw the land torn by war, with the elves, halflings, and the ancient dragons striving to protect their existence against the human onslaught. The creation of the Dragonborn, born from the sacred alliance between dragons and mortals, symbolized the resistance against humanity’s imperial ambitions.

The culmination of this era’s conflicts and the turning point in the struggle came with the founding of the Fist of Iron, a knightly order established by the god Leonus Ironmane. This order, embodying Leonus’s vision of honor and valor, united warriors from across Antæra’s diverse races to stand against the Cult of Ukrol and its human empire. After a millennium of strife, the Fist of Iron achieved victory, dismantling the Cult of Ukrol and bringing the Era of Man to a close.

The end of the Era of Man, lasting about a millennium, marked not just the cessation of human expansion but also the beginning of a new period focused on healing the wounds of war and forging a path toward mutual understanding and peace among Antæra’s races. The legacy of this era—a time of rapid human advancement at a great cost to others, followed by a united stand for freedom—remains a poignant chapter in Antæra’s history, illustrating the complex interplay of power, resistance, and the pursuit of harmony.

## 4E: The Era of the Long Winter (Frost Marches)
Transitioning from the tumultuous aftermath of the Era of Man, Antæra found itself enveloped in the clutches of a relentless winter known as the Era of the Long Winter, or more descriptively, the Frost Marches. Lasting for two millennia, this era was marked by an unprecedented freeze that reshaped the continent into an icy expanse. The onset of this ice age was the result of a sinister ambition by an ancient Lich aiming to merge the Frostfell with the Prime Material Plane. Adding to this frigid disaster was the collapse of a reality situated on the opposite side of the Far Realms, relative to Antæra, causing a dimensional upheaval that unleashed thousands of adventurers and heroes from beyond into the frozen lands of Antæra.

In the heart of this ice-bound world, the ancient tome dragon Isk’Arcaniss Vorastrix, affectionately known as Arcanis, took action. She gathered the scattered adventurers, forming the Atlas League to navigate the challenges of the Frost Marches. Arcanis anchored her floating island lair above the frosted landscape with obdurium chains, founding the city-state of Sewich as a fortress against the winter’s relentless grip.

From Sewich, the Atlas League’s members ventured forth, establishing new city-states around the Sea of Innas, thus creating the Sewich Union. This collective endeavor of the displaced adventurers fostered a network of city-states united in their struggle against the era’s challenges. To safeguard their nascent civilization from the icy wilderness and external threats, they erected a vast magical barrier, a protective dome over their region that stood as a testament to their resolve and ingenuity.

The Era of the Long Winter, or the Frost Marches, is remembered as a time of significant adversity, shaped by the elemental fury of ice and the consequences of interdimensional rifts. Yet, amidst the cold and chaos, it was also an era of unity, resilience, and survival, where the actions of a legendary dragon and the courage of adventurers from other realities laid the groundwork for a resilient civilization capable of withstanding the ice age’s harshest trials.

This era, characterized by its extreme climate and the remarkable resilience of its people, drew to a close when the artifact responsible for the Frostfell’s intrusion into the Prime Material Plane failed due to neglect and lack of maintenance. The degradation of this artifact, coupled with the lowering of the protective barrier around the Sewich Union, signaled the end of the Long Winter. As the magical dome dissipated and the ice began to retreat, Antæra slowly emerged from its frozen slumber into a period of thawing and renewal.

## 5E: The Era of Discovery (current)
Emerging from the shadows of a world once entombed in ice, Antæra welcomed the dawn of the Era of Discovery, a period marked by an awakening of curiosity and the forging of new frontiers both on the terrestrial expanse and beyond. This modern era was inaugurated by the melting of the Long Winter’s last frost, unveiling a world ripe for exploration and reconnection, not just among its native inhabitants but also with beings from realms previously beyond reach.

The advent of visitors from the Outer Planes, arriving aboard spelljammers that cut through the cosmic veil, signified a pivotal moment in Antæra’s history. These extraplanar arrivals brought with them knowledge, technology, and perspectives that catalyzed an exchange of ideas and cultures on an unprecedented scale. Spelljamming, once a fantastical notion, became the vehicle of exploration, expanding Antæra’s horizons to the infinite expanses of the cosmos and establishing it as a nexus of interplanar interaction.

This era of exploration and enlightenment, however, was juxtaposed with a deep introspection within the human communities. While many humans, particularly those outside the dominion of the New Haven Imperium, known colloquially as the Imperium, actively sought to reconcile the darker chapters of their ancestry, the Imperium itself remained entrenched in its historical practices of slavery and racism. This steadfast adherence to outdated and condemned traditions created a profound schism between the Imperium and the broader human society, highlighting a divide in the path towards redemption and progress.

The Era of Discovery thus represents a period of great potential mixed with complex societal challenges. As Antæra navigates this era, it grapples with the legacies of its past—both the darkness of division and the light of progress. The endeavors of its people to explore new frontiers and build a more inclusive world are punctuated by the internal strife within human society, highlighting the intricate dynamics of reconciliation, understanding, and the pursuit of a united future.
