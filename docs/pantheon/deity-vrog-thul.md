---
title: "Vrog’thul, Herald of Ooze"
---

- **Symbol**: A dribbling, open eye melting into slime
- **Home Plane**: Swamp of Oblivion
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolio**: Ooze, Corruption, Filth, Hunger
- **Worshipers**: Druids, sorcerers, warlocks, cultists
- **Cleric Alignments**: Chaotic Evil, Neutral Evil, Chaotic Neutral
- **Domains**: Chaos, Evil, Ooze, Slime
- **Favored Weapon**: Heavy flail

**Origins**
Vrog’thul was once a Golothoma demon, a writhing mass spawned in the deepest filth-choked trenches of the Abyss. As its influence seeped through caverns, bogs, and forsaken sewers, mortals drawn to rot and entropy began to revere it. When enough offered prayers in bile and decay, the demon’s form ruptured, and from the mire rose a new god—one born not from belief, but from infestation.

**Description**
Vrog’thul appears as a vast heap of dripping slime, eyes and mouths bubbling to the surface only to dissolve moments later. Its presence is heralded by a reek of decay and a tide of bubbling ooze that warps all it touches. Cultists claim it speaks through the sound of sucking mud or the choking gasp of a drowning rat. Shrines are often nothing more than cesspits adorned with bones and sewage-smeared relics.

**Dogma**
All things rot. All structures break down. Order is a lie, and purity is an affront. Embrace filth, for from it springs raw power. Feed the ooze with flesh, waste, and fear. Let your body melt so your soul may slither.

**Home Sphere**
No home sphere.
