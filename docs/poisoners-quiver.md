---
title: "Poisoners Quiver"
---

<div class="wd-row" markdown>
<div class="wd-cell" markdown>

The user can fill the chamber at the bottom of the quiver with 1 dose of poison, or similar substances, or other liquids inside. However, once inside, it cannot be removed. Arrows stored inside have whatever is stored in the chamber applied to them. The user does not risk exposure by retrieving arrows from the quiver.

<u>Cost</u>: 50 gp
