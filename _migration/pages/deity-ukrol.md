# Overview

- **Rank**: Greater Deity
- **Symbol**: A featureless humanoid face with a spiked crown
- **Home Plane**: Gehenna
- **Alignment**: LE
- **Portfolio**: Humans, conquest, dominion, authority
- **Worshipers**: The Imperial Clergy, the Imperial Legion, Imperial citizens, humans, the Cult of Ukrol
- **Cleric Alignments**: LE, LN, NE
- **Domains**: Domination, Hatred, Law, Tyranny
- **Favored Weapon**: Greatsword

**Origins**
Ukrol was born to a noble family in Realmspace, during the age of the Netherese Empire, on one of its many floating cities. Imperial scripture holds that he was the first perfect human and their common ancestor, made in the Abyss by a deity seeking a perfect mortal race, and the Imperium teaches this as history. Ukrol was born to a people raised as an experiment of the Netherese, bred for strength, intellect, and ambition. When he reached maturity, he was schooled in the arts of magic and the military to be an archmage among archmages. After he was deemed a success, he was sent into the stars to begin a colonization effort in the name of the Netherese Empire.

Ukrol led the humans on his vessel across the stars and into the Antæran Cluster of wildspace. Initially, attempts were made to integrate into the local populace of the spheres they landed on, though their imperialistic attitude had them chased from every world they attempted to integrate. What followed was conquest and colonization of the [Antæran Sphere](../spelljamming/sphere-antaera.md), which had not yet been exposed to Spelljamming and thus had no real defenses against it. Their plan was simple: carve out their own kingdom and build their population. Once the kingdom of the [New Haven Imperium](../nation/new-haven-imperium.md) was established, Ukrol began attempting to conquer the rest of the planet. After a long campaign he was thwarted, though the defeat bred him more worship and followers over time, leading to his eventual ascension.

As a god, Ukrol continued what he had begun as a man, seeking to bring every other race under human dominion. His campaign drove the other races of the Antæran Sphere to arms. His people were defeated and he was forced to retreat, fleeing to wildspace with a new and secret colony fleet. He continues to guide his people from the Holy City of Imperial Order in Gehenna as they expand across the stars and the planes.

**Description**
Ukrol is depicted as a figure of imposing stature, with features reminiscent of the idealized human form. His countenance exudes an air of authority and command, tempered with a sense of ambition and determination. Clad in regal attire adorned with symbols of power, Ukrol’s presence inspires awe and reverence among his followers. He is never depicted with a face in Imperial art; his symbol is a blank face beneath a spiked crown, and the faithful hold that any human face may be his.

**Dogma**
**Sovereignty**: Uphold human dominance and strive to establish human supremacy over all other races. This tenet encourages followers to assert human authority and control in all aspects of life, ensuring that humans remain the dominant force in the world.

**Expansion**: Expand human influence through territorial expansion and military conquest. Followers are urged to seek out new lands to conquer and subjugate, spreading the reach of human civilization and furthering Ukrol’s vision of human supremacy.

**Unity**: Unite the human race under a common banner and stand strong against those who oppose human dominance. This tenet emphasizes the importance of solidarity among humans, encouraging cooperation and collaboration in the pursuit of shared goals and aspirations.

**Strength**: The weak serve and the strong rule. Followers are taught that authority is earned by capability and held by force, that a leader who cannot hold his position has forfeited it, and that those unfit to lead are of better use to humanity in labor or in bondage. Ukrol’s own rise from slave to conqueror to god is taught as the proof of this tenet.

**Divine Realm**
Ukrol’s divine realm, the Holy City of Imperial Order, is a floating city in Gehenna, built in the manner of the Netherese enclave he was raised on as a child in Realmspace. The city is an inverted mountain of stone held aloft above the volcanic slopes of the plane, with the Imperial court cut into its summit and terraces of palaces, barracks, and training grounds descending its sides. The [Imperial Pantheon](../pantheons.md#the-imperial-pantheon) holds court here under his rule, and the souls of his faithful are admitted according to the rank they held in life. The city has grown with the worship of the Imperium, and new terraces are added as the population of the faithful requires.

**Clergy and Temples**
Ukrol is the head of the Imperial Pantheon, though not in public. The state temples of the Imperium are dedicated to Superbia, who holds the civic calendar and the rites every citizen attends, while the inner sanctum within the palace district of [New Haven](../settlement/imperial-capital-of-new-haven.md) is Ukrol’s and admits only his clergy and the Imperial family. A barrier erected around the sanctum repels all non-humans, which scholars outside the Imperium believe to be a misuse of a Mythal. The arrangement dates to the dormancy of the Cult after the old empire fell and was kept after the Cult’s resurrection because it had proven useful.

His clergy are charismatic leaders who preach the gospel of human dominance and the divine mandate behind the Imperium’s crusade. Most hold rank in the legions or the Imperial court, and the New Haven College of War includes instruction in his tenets. Only humans may serve him; a non-human who takes up his worship receives nothing from it. Outside the Imperium, his most devoted clergy are the [Deepfolk](../race/deepfolk.md) of the Elemental Plane of Earth, who were driven underground after the fall of the Cult and never abandoned it. Their clerics serve as both spiritual leaders and enforcers of his dogma in Deepholme, and are the only branch of the faith that has worshipped him openly without interruption.

**Rivalries**
Ukrol’s teachings and ambitions put him at odds with deities who champion the cause of equality and oppose human dominance. He views these deities as threats to his divine mandate and seeks to undermine their influence at every turn. Chief among them is [Leonus Ironmane](../pantheon/mortal-leonis.md), whose [Fist of Iron](../calendar.md) broke the first human empire and whose order remains the Imperium’s standing enemy on the continent.

Within his own pantheon, Ukrol contends with Libertas. She ascended through the worship of the humans who wished to be free from the stigma of the Imperium and rid themselves of the shame they feel toward their ancestors. Her clergy is outlawed in New Haven and tolerated in the provinces, and the Imperium has never found a means of removing her from the pantheon.

**Home Sphere**
Ukrol hails from Realmspace, a sphere beyond the [Known Spheres](../spelljamming/known-spheres.md).
