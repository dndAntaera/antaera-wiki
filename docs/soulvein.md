# Description
Located on the [Ethereal Plane](ethereal-plane.md), the radiant Soulvein weaves its mystical influence through the entire plane, resembling a sprawling, deep blue mist that dances and undulates like ethereal waves. This luminous network of magical ley-lines serves as the conduit for the essence of souls, a phenomenon known as Incarnum, that pervades the very fabric of this plane. The Soulvein itself appears as a borealis, blanketing the sky in a weaving of colors, all in different shades and hues of blue: ranging from indigo in the less saturated areas to a midnight blue in the areas with a higher saturation of Incarnum. The borealis itself follows the currents of life and death, gathering around planes where there is a higher intensity of Incarnum. This essentially functions as a map, when viewed from outside the planar boundaries, that shows the general locations of settlements.

The Soulvein is not a static phenomenon but a living essence that permeates the Ethereal Plane. Its deep blue hue, a manifestation of the myriad soul energies it contains, glows with an otherworldly brilliance that captivates the senses. As one traverses the [Ethereal Plane](ethereal-plane.md), the mist-like Soulvein becomes an ever-present companion, a celestial river of soul energy that courses through the very essence of the plane.

Wandering souls are irresistibly drawn to the Soulvein. The allure of Incarnum is like a siren's call, resonating with the remnants of past lives and the untapped potential of souls. Spirits, whether newly departed or ancient echoes from ages long past, instinctively gravitate towards the radiant mist, enticed by the promise of communion with the soul energies that have traversed the tapestry of existence.

The Soulvein serves as a gathering point for these wandering souls, forming ethereal congregations where the essence of all sentient creatures converges. It is here that the vibrancy of Incarnum becomes a beacon, attracting spirits like moths to a celestial flame. The mist deepens in color as the congregation grows, reflecting the kaleidoscope of memories, emotions, and experiences that mingle within its currents.

At the heart of the Soulvein lies the enigmatic substance known as Incarnum. It is not merely the essence of departed souls but an amorphous, magical substance that encompasses the soul energies of all sentient beings — the living, the dead, and even those yet unborn. Incarnum is the amalgamation of existence itself, a reservoir of potential and memory that transcends the boundaries of time and corporeal form.

# Theories
Among the scholars and sages of the [Ethereal Plane](ethereal-plane.md), the most commonly discussed theory is the notion that the origin of the Soulvein, and its Incarnum, is the [True Afterlife](true-afterlife.md). Whispers of this hypothesis circle among academics, but it remains unconfirmed, existing as a tantalizing enigma that boggles the minds of those who seek to unravel its mysteries. According to some sages, the Soulvein serves as a celestial river, a metaphysical tributary that carries the essence of souls from their mortal existence to the beyond. The deep blue mist that pervades the [Ethereal Plane](ethereal-plane.md) is, in this theory, a tangible manifestation of the sublime energies that transition from the Material Plane to the [True Afterlife](true-afterlife.md).

However, the conjecture remains speculative, and the [True Afterlife](true-afterlife.md) remains elusive, its existence and nature beyond the reach of empirical observation. Some sages argue that the True Afterlife might transcend the boundaries of traditional understanding, existing as a realm that defies mortal comprehension. Others caution that the ethereal currents of the Soulvein may have origins beyond the traditional dichotomy of life and death, encompassing aspects of existence that extend into unexplored realms.

Attempts to confirm or debunk this theory have proven elusive. The [True Afterlife](true-afterlife.md) remains beyond the grasp of mortal understanding. The Soulvein, while intimately connected to the ethereal journey of souls, remains a mysterious force that defies simple explanation.

Regardless of the uncertainty, the theory persists as an intellectual beacon, guiding scholars to delve deeper into the metaphysical realms of the Ethereal Plane. The Soulvein and its potential connection to the True Afterlife remain an alluring enigma, encouraging seekers of knowledge to peer into the vastness of the [Ethereal Plane](ethereal-plane.md), in hopes of unraveling the celestial secrets that lie beyond mortal comprehension.

# Incarnum & Meldshapers
In the intricate art of meldshaping, practitioners harness the enigmatic substance known as Incarnum to sculpt and manipulate soul energy into tangible forms. Whether they are aware of it or not, these meldshapers are tapping into the radiant mist of the Soulvein, drawing upon its currents to shape the magical essence that permeates the [Ethereal Plane](ethereal-plane.md).

Incarnum, this amorphous magical substance composed of the essence of all sentient beings, is essentially the refined soul energy coursing through the veins of the Soulvein. Meldshapers, through their craft, channel and shape this ethereal energy into a variety of forms, creating everything from armor and weapons to constructs and artifacts. What they might not realize is that the source of their power is deeply entwined with the expansive currents of the Soulvein.

Whether knowingly or unknowingly, meldshapers act as conduits for the Soulvein's radiant mist, tapping into its rich reservoir of soul energy to fuel their creations. The process involves manipulating Incarnum, and in doing so, meldshapers are essentially navigating the ethereal currents of the Soulvein, drawing upon its vast well of potential to bring their melds to life.

Those who delve knowingly into the Soulvein understand the profound connection between their craft and the ethereal mist. They might actively seek out specific ley lines or congregations along the Soulvein, intuitively selecting areas where the energies are particularly potent. These adept meldshapers might even weave rituals or invocations that resonate with the essence of the Soulvein, enhancing the potency of their melds.

On the other hand, some meldshapers might be entirely unaware of the source of their power. Their innate ability to manipulate Incarnum could be an unconscious tapping into the ambient energy of the Soulvein, a skill honed through intuition and practice rather than explicit knowledge. These individuals might find themselves naturally drawn to areas where the Soulvein's currents are strong, unknowingly aligning their meldshaping with the ethereal flow.

In essence, meldshapers, knowingly or not, become artisans of the Soulvein, weaving the essence of souls into tangible forms. The Soulvein becomes the unseen forge, supplying the raw material for their melding artistry, and meldshapers, like skilled artisans, shape and mold the radiant mist into manifestations of ethereal beauty.
