# Overview

Concordia is the capital sphere of the [New Haven Imperium](../nation/new-haven-imperium.md) and lies beyond the [Known Spheres](known-spheres.md). It is a standard sphere of seven bodies in orbit around the primary Invictus. Its crystal shell lies 1,010 million miles from the primary and holds four known portals, each held by a fortress station of the Imperial fleet. The phlo current that reaches it appears on no chart of the Known Spheres, and the Imperium has not shared its course. The sphere was taken by the fleet [Ukrol](../deity/ukrol.md) led out of the [Antæran Sphere](sphere-antaera.md) at the close of the 3rd Era and was unknown to the Known Spheres until the [holy war](../nation/new-haven-imperium.md#the-holy-war). Its native populations, human and otherwise, were conquered world by world across the 4th Era.

The primary body, Invictus, is a manifest zone to the Quasi-Elemental Plane of Radiance. Its light is white and constant, and it has not been observed to dim in any record the Imperium keeps. The intensity of the zone washes out the color of the planets in its sphere, and from wildspace every body in the system reads as pale or white regardless of its surface. The same light bleaches the color from its inhabitants over a lifetime of exposure, and the skin of those born under it takes on a pallor similar to albinism. This change is gradual, having taken generations to manifest in the inhabitants. [Planar Crystals](../item/planar-crystal.md) of fire manifest in orbit around the primary body, free-floating in wildspace rather than fixed to any surface, a result of the intensity and purity of the radiant energy the manifest zone emits.

The wildspace of Concordia carries several anomalies attributed to the primary. Bands of concentrated light cross the sphere at irregular intervals, bright enough to blind an unshielded helmsman and to burn the paint from a hull in a single passage, and the Imperial fleet charts their drift daily. Fields of bleached debris orbit between the worlds, the remains of asteroids and derelicts stripped of color down to a uniform white, and sailors use them as waymarkers because nothing else in the sphere stands out against them. Shadows within the sphere fall shorter and paler than the geometry of the light accounts for, and in the inner tracks they do not fall at all.

A large planar conduit lies within the [Ethereal Plane](../plane/ethereal-plane.md) that overlaps the sphere. It opens onto Gehenna and ends at a massive gate within the Holy City of Imperial Order, Ukrol's domain city. The Imperium holds, by prophecy, that Ukrol placed the conduit there himself so that his faithful could find his afterlife more easily, and the dead of the Imperium are committed with rites that direct them to it. Vessels entering the Ethereal from Concordia report the conduit as a column of white light visible from any point in the overlap.

The [Imperial Pantheon](../pantheons.md#the-imperial-pantheon) has its seat in the sphere, and Ukrol is worshipped openly here. Outsiders are not permitted to settle in Concordia, and those admitted on diplomatic passage travel from the shell to Dominium and back along a single approved course.

# Planetology & Astrology

- Type of Sphere: Standard
- Primary Body:
    - Name: Invictus
    - Type: Fire Body (Manifest zone to the Quasi-Elemental Plane of Radiance)
- Spelljamming Aware: Yes

## Inner Track (<200 million miles)

- **Planet Name**: Carcer
    - **Distance**: 58 million miles
    - **Size Category**: B
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: The first body in the celestial track is Carcer. A planet-wide prison for criminals and prisoners of the Imperium. The world sits close enough to the primary that the radiance on its surface is intense enough to blister exposed skin within hours and to blind within days. The side facing the sun averages 100 to 110 degrees, and the night averages 30 to 50. There are no walls on Carcer and none are needed. The prisoners are landed with rations and left under the open sky, and most who are sent here die of exposure, either to the light itself or to the oppression it represents, before their sentence is counted out. Those who survive the planet develop a thick, leather-like skin in addition to the bleaching the light works on every inhabitant of the sphere. Convicts who last are often recruited into a special warfare unit of the Legion and sent on the harshest of its missions on account of their resilience. The Imperium keeps a single garrison on the world to receive the transports, and it records every death as a sentence completed.

- **Planet Name**: Castra
    - **Distance**: 131 million miles
    - **Size Category**: D
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Ring
    - **Other Notes**: The second body in the celestial track is Castra. A world dedicated to the legions. The body is a ring, and gravity on it is strange and relative: the inside face of the ring is the ground, and anything standing on it is held there with the sky at the ring's center. At that center sits a stronghold, held aloft by the gravitational pull of the ring around it, which garrisons the magical department of the Legion. The outer crust of the ring, the uninhabited outer shell, is covered in naturally occurring basalt flats that absorb the heat of the primary and distribute it through the body of the ring, which keeps the inner face warm through the night. The divisions of the Legion's troops are garrisoned along the inner face in large cities made up of government-funded housing and facilities, with parade grounds, drill fields, and ranges laid between them and the senior campus of the College of War among them. Every citizen of Concordia is sent here at the age of majority to serve their term. No one lives on Castra who is not in training, instructing, or garrisoning it.

## Goldilocks Zone (200–300 million miles)

- **Planet Name**: Dominium
    - **Distance**: 236 million miles
    - **Size Category**: E
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: The third body in the celestial track is Dominium. The capital world of the Imperium and the seat of its government. Its cities are planned to a grid and built in white stone, each one a copy of the last, with the Imperial seat, the Curia, and the great temple of Ukrol at the center of the largest. Every citizen holds a residence, a ration, and a post. The streets are clean and the markets are full, and the labor that keeps them so is performed before dawn by slaves who are quartered outside the walls and admitted by gate pass. Dominium holds the census of the sphere, and no record is amended anywhere in Concordia without a copy entered here.

- **Planet Name**: Hortus
    - **Distance**: 284 million miles
    - **Size Category**: D
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: The fourth body in the celestial track is Hortus. A world dedicated to agriculture. It feeds the sphere and is worked entirely by slaves, who outnumber the overseers posted there by a hundred to one. Its surface is farmland, pasture, and orchard from coast to coast, divided into estates held by noble houses that do not reside on them. The non-human population of Concordia is confined to Hortus and to the labor quarters of the other worlds, and a citizen may live a full life in the sphere without seeing one. The world has no cities, and its only garrison is a chain of fortified granaries along the equator.

## Outer Track (>300 million miles)

- **Planet Name**: Officina
    - **Distance**: 352 million miles
    - **Size Category**: D
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: The fifth body in the celestial track is Officina. A world dedicated to manufacture. It holds the fleet yards of the Imperium, the foundries that arm them, and the powder works where firearms are made. It was placed this far from Dominium so that an accident in the powder works reaches nothing the Imperium values. Its atmosphere is thin and cold and its cities are enclosed. Officina's workforce is mixed: artisan citizens hold the yards and the gun shops, slaves hold the mines and the mills, and the two do not share a quarter.

- **Planet Name**: Limes
    - **Distance**: 421 million miles
    - **Size Category**: A
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Belt
    - **Other Notes**: The sixth body in the celestial track is Limes. A belt of asteroids fortified across its full circumference, which the Imperium holds to be the border of its territory within the sphere. Every rock of size carries a battery or a watch station, and the fleet patrols the gaps. Mining is carried out in the belt by slave crews under garrison. It was within the approach to Limes that the Gallamarketh colony ship was hailed, failed to answer, and was destroyed.

- **Planet Name**: Silentium
    - **Distance**: 497 million miles
    - **Size Category**: B
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Planet Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: The seventh body in the celestial track is Silentium. A frozen world at the edge of the sphere, which the census lists as holding a weather observatory and a staff of forty. It holds the Imperium's political prisoners, those who accused it of the Mythal at New Haven and did not die of natural causes, and those whose records have been removed from the census. No vessel is scheduled to Silentium and none is recorded as having returned from it. The Imperium does not discuss the world and does not deny that it exists.
