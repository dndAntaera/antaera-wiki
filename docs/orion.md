# Orion, patron of Smallfolk

**Domains**: Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Protection

**Appearance**:
In his divine form, Orion is often depicted or visualized as a humanoid figure radiating with astral light, his body seemingly made of the same cosmic material as his original crystalline form. He is sometimes shown with facets of crystal jutting from his form, a reminder of his origins.

**Backstory**:
Orion began his existence not as a deity, but as a supermassive [planar crystal](planar-crystal.md) located in the heart of Antæra. This crystal was no ordinary formation; it was deeply attuned to the energies and the very essence of the [Astral Plane](astral-plane.md). Over millennia, this crystal absorbed the surrounding cosmic forces, which led to a profound transformation. As the crystal absorbed more astral energy, it began to awaken, slowly developing a consciousness. This was a gradual process, where the crystal transitioned from an inanimate object into a sentient being. Its awareness of the cosmos and the planes grew, and with this awareness came an understanding of its place in the universe.

Orion's burgeoning consciousness and growing power eventually led to his ascension. The crystal, now a sentient being, transcended its physical form and became a divine entity. This transformation was marked by an increase in power and a deepening connection to the [Astral Plane](astral-plane.md) and the universe at large.

**Divine Realm**:
Orion's divine realm is a magnificent crystalline sanctuary in the [Astral Plane](astral-plane.md), echoing his origins. It's a realm of harmonious energy, filled with celestial light and floating crystal formations.

**Tenets of Orion**:

*Seek knowledge and harmony with the cosmos*
This tenet emphasizes the pursuit of understanding and cosmic awareness. Followers of Orion are encouraged to explore the mysteries of the universe and seek a deeper connection with the astral energies that pervade all existence. This pursuit is not just intellectual but spiritual, promoting a sense of unity with the cosmos. It involves studying the stars, delving into astral phenomena, and understanding the interconnectedness of all beings and planes. Orion's followers believe that true wisdom comes from comprehending the vastness of the universe and finding one's place within it.

*Protect and cherish the smallfolk and their creations*
Orion's second tenet is a call to safeguard and value the smaller races and their contributions. It's a directive to defend the smallfolk - gnomes, halflings, dwarves, and others - from threats and to honor their cultures and achievements. This tenet is deeply rooted in respect for the creativity, resilience, and potential of these races. It extends to the protection of their lands, homes, and ways of life. Clerics and followers of Orion are often seen as guardians and advocates for smallfolk communities, ensuring their safety and providing guidance and support.

*Foster community, cooperation, and prosperity among all beings*
This tenet is about building and maintaining strong, supportive communities. Orion's teachings encourage collaboration and mutual aid, not just among the smallfolk but across all races and cultures. The emphasis is on working together for the common good, creating an environment where all can thrive. This tenet promotes peace, understanding, and shared prosperity. It speaks to the heart of community living - helping others, sharing resources, and building strong bonds. Orion's followers often engage in communal projects, diplomatic endeavors, and any activities that strengthen the bonds between different peoples and cultures.

**Holy Symbol**:
Orion's symbols are often those associated with the cosmos and the smallfolk he patronizes. These might include a crystal radiating astral light or a simple figure surrounded by the stars of the [Astral Plane](astral-plane.md).

**Clergy and Temples**:
Orion's clergy are often smallfolk themselves, serving as guardians of knowledge and community leaders. His temples are beautifully crafted from crystal and stone, designed to reflect the celestial beauty of the [Astral Plane](astral-plane.md), and are often found in smallfolk communities.

**Rivalries**:
Orion's primary rivalries are with deities who threaten the well-being and prosperity of the smallfolk. This includes gods of destruction and those who overlook or disdain the smaller races. He stands as a steadfast guardian against any forces that seek to disrupt the harmony and growth of his creations.
