# Eras

## 1E: The Mythical Era
Marked by the arrival of the Eladrin on the Prime Material Plane in the region later named the Mir'raask Gulf, this epoch heralds the inception of what is known today by archaeologists as "Ancient Antæra." These trailblazers, the "Ancient Antærans," engineered a civilization of profound influence, shrouded in the mysteries of its origin. The reasons for their departure from the Feywild are veiled in obscurity, with no surviving records to elucidate this monumental exodus. Fragments of ancient texts, scarce yet invaluable, offer glimpses into their migration and the indelible mark it left on the world. These texts, coupled with the awe-inspiring architectural relics scattered across the globe, testify to the Ancient Antærans' sophisticated mastery of construction and magic.

As enigmatic as its beginning, the conclusion of the Mythical Era is defined by the sudden, unexplained disappearance of the Ancient Antærans. Their advanced society, known for its widespread architectural wonders, mysteriously faded, leaving no trace of their whereabouts. This baffling end to the Ancient Antærans' chapter has remained a source of intrigue and speculation among scholars. The enduring remnants of their constructions stand as silent witnesses to a bygone age of discovery, innovation, and enigma.

It is currently believed by scholars that Vortressa, who is now recognized as the goddess of monsters, was originally an ambitious mage during the Mythical Era. Her quest for power led her to explore the darker side  of summoning, through which she introduced a myriad of monsters and other formidable creatures from distant realities into Antæra. These creatures, with their varied and terrifying forms, became a significant part of Antæra’s ecosystem, shaping its landscapes and legends. As these monstrous beings spread across the continent, their unwavering loyalty and worship towards Vortressa provided her with a unique source of divine power. This worship by her monstrous creations eventually elevated her to godhood, establishing Vortressa as a deity who is linked to the very horrors she summoned into the world.

## 2E: The Era of Emergences
In the initial dawn of the Era of Emergences, the world was graced with the divine creation of the smallfolk races by Orion. Gnomes emerged from the radiance of Planar Crystals, each variant reflecting the stone's unique magic. From the world's deepest ores, the dwarves were shaped, as enduring and valuable as the metals from which they were formed. Halflings, born from the earth's welcoming clay, embodied adaptability and a profound connection to life. This burst of creation infused the world with diversity and potential, marking a vibrant beginning for these burgeoning societies.

However, this diversity also sowed seeds of division among the gnomes, leading to the Wars of Crystal Discord. This era of strife showcased the gnomes' ingenuity but also highlighted their internal conflicts, affecting the broader tapestry of smallfolk communities. In response to the turmoil, dwarves sought refuge in the earth's depths, crafting splendid subterranean kingdoms, while halflings found kinship and peace within elven societies, demonstrating their inherent adaptability.

The conclusion of the Era of Emergences was heralded by the elves' intervention to quell the gnome conflicts. Their wise and decisive actions brought an end to the discord, paving the way for a period of healing and reconciliation. This pivotal moment, facilitated by the elves, not only ended the hostilities but also marked the beginning of a new chapter focused on rebuilding and fostering unity among the races.

The Era of Emergences, with its remarkable beginnings, challenging conflicts, and transformative resolutions, laid the foundations for a world rich in culture and communal harmony. The elves' peacemaking efforts underscored the era's end, ushering in a legacy of cooperation and understanding that would influence the development of interconnected societies, celebrating the diversity and resilience of the world's inhabitants.

The Era of Emergences spanned approximately five centuries, a testament to the enduring impact of its events and the lasting legacy of the races it brought forth. This period of time, though but a chapter in the world's expansive history, remains a critical juncture that shaped the societal, cultural, and political landscapes that followed.

## 3E: The Era of Man
Spanning approximately 1000 years, the Era of Man was initiated by a monumental exodus led by Ukrol, who guided humanity from a distant reality into Antæra, settling the fertile Antæran Plains. Under Ukrol’s divine directive, humans quickly established an expansive, imperialistic empire with the intention of dominating the continent’s native races. This era of human ascendancy, marked by their swift development and expansion, posed a unique terror to the longer-lived races such as elves and halflings. The rapid pace at which humans could learn, adapt, and grow, compounded by their shorter life spans, made their empire’s spread both relentless and devastating.

The conflict that ensued engulfed the continent, as the human empire sought to subjugate or exterminate those who opposed their dominion. This dark chapter saw the land torn by war, with the elves, halflings, and the ancient dragons striving to protect their existence against the human onslaught. The creation of the Dragonborn, born from the sacred alliance between dragons and mortals, symbolized the resistance against humanity’s imperial ambitions.

The culmination of this era’s conflicts and the turning point in the struggle came with the founding of the Fist of Iron, a knightly order established by the deity Leonus Ironmane. This order, embodying Leonus’s vision of honor and valor, united warriors from across Antæra’s diverse races to stand against the Cult of Ukrol and its human empire. After a millennium of strife, the Fist of Iron achieved victory, dismantling the Cult of Ukrol and bringing the Era of Man to a close.

The end of the Era of Man, lasting about a millennium, marked not just the cessation of human expansion but also the beginning of a new period focused on healing the wounds of war and forging a path toward mutual understanding and peace among Antæra’s races. The legacy of this era—a time of rapid human advancement at a great cost to others, followed by a united stand for freedom—remains a poignant chapter in Antæra’s history, illustrating the complex interplay of power, resistance, and the pursuit of harmony.

## 4E: The Era of the Long Winter (Frost Marches)
Transitioning from the tumultuous aftermath of the Era of Man, Antæra found itself enveloped in the clutches of a relentless winter known as the Era of the Long Winter, or more descriptively, the Frost Marches. Lasting for two millennia, this era was marked by an unprecedented freeze that reshaped the continent into an icy expanse. The onset of this ice age was the result of a sinister ambition by an ancient Lich aiming to merge the Frostfell with the Prime Material Plane. Adding to this frigid disaster was the collapse of a reality situated on the opposite side of the Far Realms, relative to Antæra, causing a dimensional upheaval that unleashed thousands of adventurers and heroes from beyond into the frozen lands of Antæra.

In the heart of this ice-bound world, the ancient tome dragon Isk’Arcaniss Vorastrix, affectionately known as Arcanis, took action. She gathered the scattered adventurers, forming the Atlas League to navigate the challenges of the Frost Marches. Arcanis anchored her floating island lair above the frosted landscape with obdurium chains, founding the city-state of Sewich as a fortress against the winter’s relentless grip.

From Sewich, the Atlas League’s members ventured forth, establishing new city-states around the Sea of Innas, thus creating the Sewich Union. This collective endeavor of the displaced adventurers fostered a network of city-states united in their struggle against the era’s challenges. To safeguard their nascent civilization from the icy wilderness and external threats, they erected a vast magical barrier, a protective dome over their region that stood as a testament to their resolve and ingenuity.

The Era of the Long Winter, or the Frost Marches, is remembered as a time of significant adversity, shaped by the elemental fury of ice and the consequences of interdimensional rifts. Yet, amidst the cold and chaos, it was also an era of unity, resilience, and survival, where the actions of a legendary dragon and the courage of adventurers from other realities laid the groundwork for a resilient civilization capable of withstanding the ice age’s harshest trials.

This era, characterized by its extreme climate and the remarkable resilience of its people, drew to a close when the artifact responsible for the Frostfell’s intrusion into the Prime Material Plane failed due to neglect and lack of maintenance. The degradation of this artifact, coupled with the lowering of the protective barrier around the Sewich Union, signaled the end of the Long Winter. As the magical dome dissipated and the ice began to retreat, Antæra slowly emerged from its frozen slumber into a period of thawing and renewal.

## 5E: The Era of Discovery (current)
Emerging from the shadows of a world once entombed in ice, Antæra welcomed the dawn of the Era of Discovery, a period marked by an awakening of curiosity and the forging of new frontiers both on the terrestrial expanse and beyond. This modern era was inaugurated by the melting of the Long Winter’s last frost, unveiling a world ripe for exploration and reconnection, not just among its native inhabitants but also with beings from realms previously beyond reach.

The advent of visitors from the Outer Planes, arriving aboard spelljammers that cut through the cosmic veil, signified a pivotal moment in Antæra’s history. These extraplanar arrivals brought with them knowledge, technology, and perspectives that catalyzed an exchange of ideas and cultures on an unprecedented scale. Spelljamming, once a fantastical notion, became the vehicle of exploration, expanding Antæra’s horizons to the infinite expanses of the cosmos and establishing it as a nexus of interplanar interaction.

This era of exploration and enlightenment, however, was juxtaposed with a deep introspection within the human communities. While many humans, particularly those outside the dominion of the New Haven Imperium, known colloquially as the Imperium, actively sought to reconcile the darker chapters of their ancestry, the Imperium itself remained entrenched in its historical practices of slavery and racism. This steadfast adherence to outdated and condemned traditions created a profound schism between the Imperium and the broader human society, highlighting a divide in the path towards redemption and progress.

The Era of Discovery thus represents a period of great potential mixed with complex societal challenges. As Antæra navigates this era, it grapples with the legacies of its past—both the darkness of division and the light of progress. The endeavors of its people to explore new frontiers and build a more inclusive world are punctuated by the internal strife within human society, highlighting the intricate dynamics of reconciliation, understanding, and the pursuit of a united future.
