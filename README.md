# Antæra Wiki

The reference wiki for **Antæra**, a Dungeons & Dragons setting: a world
emerging from an age of ice, its kingdoms reawakening, and the wildspace
beyond it.

**Read it at [dndantaera.github.io/antaera-wiki](https://dndantaera.github.io/antaera-wiki/)**

## What is in it

- **The Cosmology** and the planes that surround the world
- **The Pantheons** — the gods, their heralds, and their mortal ascendants
- **Spelljamming** — arcane space, ships, and the Known Spheres
- **Character Creation** and the houserules collected in The Index
- Regions, cities, factions and peoples, indexed A–Z in the **Glossary**

Pages marked *work in progress* are still being written. Retired material,
including the 2014 Stellar Marches campaign, is kept under **Archived Pages**.

## Editing

Content is edited in the browser at [Pages CMS](https://app.pagescms.org),
which works on desktop and mobile. Saving publishes the change; the site
rebuilds itself within a minute.

Pages are plain Markdown, so they can also be edited directly in this
repository.
