# Origin Story
In the annals of Antæra's ancient history, the arrival of the elven people stands as a pivotal chapter, shrouded in the mists of time and resonating with a profound sense of otherworldly origins.

Long ago, a group of Eladrin, beings of enchanting beauty and fey magic, embarked on a celestial exodus from their ancestral realm, the Sidhe Wilderness. Their journey through the cosmic tapestry guided them to the Material Plane, where destiny called them to a specific destination—an island nestled in the southeastern expanse of Antæra. Separated from their fey homeland, the Eladrin arrived on this untouched, idyllic island, untouched by mortal presence. It was a place of unparalleled beauty, where emerald forests stretched towards cerulean skies, and crystalline waters kissed the shores. Here, on this secluded paradise, they would lay the foundations of their new home.

As the Eladrin settled into their newfound abode, something profound began to transpire. The fey magic that had once defined them as denizens of the Sidhe Wilderness began to wane. Over the span of thousands of years, they underwent a gradual transformation, their fey essence merging with the earthly realm. Slowly but surely, they evolved from ethereal Eladrin into true mortals.

Though the Eladrin lost their innate connection to the Sidhe Wilderness, one gift from their ancestral realm remained—extended lifespans. The elves, as they came to be known, found themselves gifted with long lives that stretched far beyond the years of other mortal races. They began to view the world through the prism of centuries, perceiving the ebb and flow of time as an eternal river, carrying them through its currents.

As the elves flourished on their island sanctuary, their insatiable curiosity led them beyond its shores. They embarked on journeys of exploration and colonization, venturing into the uncharted realms of Antæra. With each new territory they claimed, they forged connections with the land, absorbing its energies and planar influences. Over time, the diverse landscapes and planar forces surrounding them birthed a multitude of subcultures within elven society. Those who settled amidst ancient forests became attuned to nature, their physical appearances reflecting their deep bond with the wild. Elves dwelling near elemental planes underwent transformations, adapting to the elemental forces that permeated their surroundings.

As they spread across Antæra, the elves continued to evolve, their physical traits and temperaments molded by their environments. The result was a culture as diverse as the landscapes themselves, a tapestry of interconnected but distinct elven subcultures. The origins of the elven race in Antæra bear witness to their enduring adaptability and insatiable thirst for understanding the Material Plane. While their connection to the Sidhe Wilderness faded into myth, they emerged as the first true mortals of Antæra, forever bound to its ever-changing landscapes and planar influences. Their legacy endures, a testament to their role as stewards of the world's mysteries and as guardians of its ancient cultures.

# General Culture
In the sprawling world of Antæra, the elven culture stands as a living testament to ancient origins, interplanar travel, and harmonious cooperation with the mystical forces that shape their existence. Rooted in traditions that span countless millennia, the elves have woven a rich tapestry (apologies for the repetition) of society, magic, and memory. The elves of Antæra, with their ancient heritage and enduring traditions, stand as a beacon of wisdom and mysticism in the ever-evolving world around them. Their commitment to interplanar harmony and their role as keepers of memories ensure that their legacy endures for countless generations to come, a testament to the enduring power of their culture in shaping the destiny of Antæra.

**Ancient Eladrin Lineage**:
Elven culture traces its lineage back to the Eladrin, ancient beings who immigrated from the mystical Sidhe Wilderness and established a presence on the Material Plane. These progenitors spread across the land, dominating it for hundreds of thousands of years before the emergence of other mortal races. The elves see themselves as the original inhabitants of Antæra, destined to guide its destiny.

**Society of Bloodlines**:
The societal structure of the elves is built upon the concept of bloodlines and ancestral memories. Each elf carries within them the collective memories of their forebears, providing a unique perspective on their cultural history. These ancestral memories are accessible during their sleep trance, allowing them to draw upon the wisdom and experiences of generations past. This shared consciousness forms the bedrock of elven society, binding them together in a web of familial connections and shared knowledge.

**Elders of Wisdom**:
At the pinnacle of elven hierarchy are the eldest among them, individuals who have accrued centuries, if not millennia, of memories. These venerable elves are revered for their unparalleled wisdom and hold positions of great influence within their society. They are the keepers of ancient lore, the custodians of traditions, and the ultimate arbitrators of disputes. Their wisdom guides the path of their people, ensuring that the lessons of the past shape the future.

**Masters of Interplanar Magic**:
Many elves find their calling as mages specializing in interplanar magic. They forge pacts and strike deals with outsiders, fostering cooperation and alliances with beings from other realms. This practice is deeply ingrained in elven culture, shaping their architecture and technology. Crystals, attuned to various planes, are seamlessly integrated into their structures, serving as conduits for their interplanar magics and adding an otherworldly beauty to their creations. These intricate crystal patterns are not just aesthetic marvels but also functional, enabling communication and transport across the multiverse.

**Aloof Mystique**:
To those with shorter lifespans, elves may appear aloof and enigmatic, for they view events and goals through the lens of centuries and even millennia. Their longevity allows them to perceive the world in a deliberate and unhurried manner, which can be mistaken for slothfulness or disinterest. However, this measured perspective is a testament to their profound connection to the flow of time itself. They recognize the ebb and flow of civilizations, knowing that what may seem pressing to a shorter-lived race is but a fleeting moment to them.

**Guardians of Interplanar Harmony**:
Elven culture in Antæra revolves around the principles of interplanar harmony and cooperation. Their deep ties to the fey and outsiders, their reverence for ancestral memories, and their mastery of interplanar magic all contribute to their unique identity as the guardians of this delicate balance in the multiverse. They view themselves as stewards of the connections between planes, ensuring that alliances endure and conflicts are resolved peacefully.

# Subculture: The Drow

In the depths of the Earthspire Expanse, a subculture of elves known as the Drow has thrived in obscurity and secrecy. These enigmatic beings have carved out a shadowy existence, marked by their unique physical attributes and a culture steeped in darkness and mystery.

The Drow, with their otherworldly beauty and insidious practices, remain a complex and multifaceted subculture within the vast tapestry of Antæra. Their subterranean existence, radical ideologies, and relentless pursuit of dominance have left an indelible mark on the world, casting them as both enigmatic enchanters and ruthless oppressors in the eyes of those who dare to venture into the depths of the Earthspire Expanse.

**Appearance**:
The Drow exhibit a strikingly distinct appearance compared to their surface-dwelling kin. Their skin, a testament to their subterranean existence, ranges from deep, velvety blues to rich, regal purples. This unusual pigmentation is a direct consequence of the ever-present miasma that permeates their underground domain, lending an ethereal and otherworldly quality to their complexion.

Perhaps the most captivating aspect of the Drow's appearance is their eyes, which shimmer with varying hues of mesmerizing crimson. These eyes possess an iridescent quality, appearing as though they hold secrets untold. The radiance of their gaze seems to dance like flickering flames in the darkness of their underground realm, a reflection of the fiery passions and ambitions that fuel their hearts.

Completing their striking visage is their hair, which ranges from pure, moonlit white to shimmering silver. This silvery crown, cascading like ethereal waterfalls, stands in stark contrast to their dark and subterranean surroundings. It serves as a silent testament to their enigmatic origins and the influence of the Earthspire Expanse's miasma on their physical form.

**Cruel Dominion**:
The Drow are infamous for their oppressive dominion over the various races inhabiting the Earthspire Expanse. Their society is marked by a hierarchical structure, with the Drow at the apex, wielding power and control over those beneath them. Enslavement is a common practice among the Drow, and they view subjugation as a means to assert their dominance and secure their place in the depths.

**Ancient Enmity**:
The Drow's antagonistic relationship with the Deepfolk, another subculture of humans dwelling in the Earthspire Expanse, is a long-standing feud that has persisted through generations. Warfare, subterfuge, and ceaseless conflict define their interactions, as both races vie for control of the subterranean realm they call home.

**Radical Isolationists**:
Originally, the Drow were high elves who departed from the main elven civilization due to irreconcilable political differences. Over time, their isolation in the Earthspire Expanse nurtured their radical beliefs, intensifying their desires for power and dominance. Their once-moderate ideology grew increasingly extreme, eventually culminating in their transformation into one of the most feared and reviled mortal races in the known world.
