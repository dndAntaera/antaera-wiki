---
title: "Ukrol, Patron Deity of Humanity"
---

**Domains**: Domination, Hatred, Law, Tyrrany

**Appearance**:

Ukrol is depicted as a figure of imposing stature, with features reminiscent of the idealized human form. His countenance exudes an air of authority and command, tempered with a sense of ambition and determination. Clad in regal attire adorned with symbols of power, Ukrol's presence inspires awe and reverence among his followers.

**Backstory**:

Ukrol's origins are shrouded in mystery, but legends speak of him as the first human, born from the depths of the Abyss itself. It is said that an Abyssal god sought to create the perfect race of mortals, and Ukrol was the culmination of this divine experiment. Endowed with incredible strength, intelligence, and ambition, Ukrol quickly rose to prominence among his kind, leading them with unmatched fervor and determination.

Driven by a desire to prove the superiority of his race, Ukrol embarked on a relentless campaign of conquest, seeking to bring all other beings under human dominion. His actions sparked conflict and upheaval across the planes as other races rose up to resist his tyranny. Despite facing countless adversaries, Ukrol's conviction never wavered, and he led his people with unwavering resolve.

However, as Ukrol's empire expanded, so too did the opposition against him. Faced with the combined might of his enemies, Ukrol's forces were eventually defeated, and he and his followers were cast out from their own Prime Material Plane as punishment for their crimes against other races. Undeterred by their exile, Ukrol and his followers embarked on a perilous journey across the planes, seeking a new world where they could realize their dreams of human supremacy. They eventually settled on a different Prime Material Plane, Antæra, and began their conquest anew.

**Divine Realm**:

Ukrol's divine realm, known as the Dominion of Exiles, is a realm shrouded in shadows and echoes of past glory. Crumbling citadels and ruined monuments serve as reminders of Ukrol's former reign, now lost to the ravages of time and exile. It is a place of bitter nostalgia and unfulfilled ambition, where the echoes of Ukrol's past conquests linger like ghosts.

**Tenets of Ukrol**:

*Sovereignty*: Uphold human dominance and strive to establish human supremacy over all other races. This tenet encourages followers to assert human authority and control in all aspects of life, ensuring that humans remain the dominant force in the world.

*Expansion*: Expand human influence through territorial expansion and military conquest. Followers are urged to seek out new lands to conquer and subjugate, spreading the reach of human civilization and furthering Ukrol's vision of human supremacy.

*Unity*: Unite the human race under a common banner and stand strong against those who oppose human dominance. This tenet emphasizes the importance of solidarity among humans, encouraging cooperation and collaboration in the pursuit of shared goals and aspirations.

**Holy Symbol**:

The symbol of Ukrol is a featureless humanoid face with a spiked crown, representing human strength, dominion, and the crown of authority. It serves as a potent emblem of Ukrol's power and the aspirations of his followers for human supremacy.

**Clergy and Temples**:

Temples dedicated to Ukrol can be found in human settlements across Antæra, serving as centers of worship and indoctrination for his followers. His clergy are often charismatic leaders who preach the gospel of human dominance and seek to unite their followers under Ukrol's banner.

**Rivalries**:

Ukrol's teachings and ambitions put him at odds with deities who champion the cause of equality and oppose human dominance. He views these deities as threats to his divine mandate and seeks to undermine their influence at every turn.
