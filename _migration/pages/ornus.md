# Overview

- **Rank**: Intermediate Deity
- **Symbol**: A gear set within a perfect square
- **Home Plane**: Mechanus
- **Alignment**: LN
- **Portfolio**: Constructs, order, artifice, creation
- **Worshipers**: Artificers, golemwrights, smiths, engineers, constructs
- **Cleric Alignments**: LE, LG, LN
- **Domains**: Artifice, Construct, Law, Inevitable
- **Favored Weapon**: Light Hammer

**Origins**
Before he was the Lord of Constructs, Ornus was the Primus on Mechanus: the supreme ruler of the Modrons and a being with godlike powers on Mechanus. In normal circumstances, the position of Primus is the pinnacle of Modron society: once a Primus falls, the Secundus takes their place within the hour and the entire hierarchy shifts up. However, with the advent of modern artifice being spread throughout the [Known Spheres](../spelljamming/known-spheres.md), the collective worship of these artificers ascended him to true divinity. As he ascended, another Primus took his place and the hierarchy itself shifted with Ornus being the new pinnacle, claiming all constructs as his domain. This was done without his own will, though he was pleasantly surprised when his godlike power began to extend further than Mechanus itself.

**Description**
Upon ascension, Ornus took on the form of a plain humanoid devoid of features. His body appears to be made of brass, though this is in appearance only, as his godly endurance would attest otherwise. His face, too, is devoid of features save for the singular gear set in the middle of where his face would be, spinning in constant motion with exception only for telepathic speech. His only avatar is the Primus itself, having been elevated in its own right to adopt the position of avatar and gaining new divinely inspired powers. The Inevitables created in Mechanus are his arms throughout the multiverse, meting out his justice and enforcing his order upon all those who would defy Law and Order.

**Dogma**
**Intention Behind Construction**: Constructs should be orderly and built with intention in each function. To add the erroneous or redundant is heresy.

**To Make Is To Owe Maintenance**: A creator who does not maintain or take care of his creations is a blasphemer, and a heretic to the Law of creation. He who abandons his projects should be abandoned himself.

**Metal Is Eternal**: Faithful who are of flesh should strive to join with the perfection of metal and machinery, for time continues not for those who follow clockwork. Metal endures while the flesh is weak and rots.

**Divine Realm**
Ornus created a new realm for himself within the heart of Mechanus itself, choosing to be one with Law and Order rather than to expose himself to Chaos and Discord. The scarce times he chooses to leave his realm are when he must deliver mandates to his Primus or observe the Modron March, which presses on consistently.

**Clergy and Temples**
The hierarchy of the clergy mimics the hierarchy of Modron society, viewing it to be a perfect analogy for all things Lawful and Order. Advancement in the clergy is determined by merit and skill, and is usually taken up by Forge Clerics and Artificers. Though rare, Wizards who dabble in the creation of constructs or golems choose to worship Ornus. Fewer still are intelligent constructs: those who have awoken and answered the call of Ornus to serve the clergy and their fellow constructs. In the off chance that a [Warforged](../anthropology/warforged.md) decides to join the clergy, they are welcomed with open arms. Though the clergy of Ornus does not go out of its way to try and recruit Warforged, they allow them to join should they choose to do so of their own volition. Occasionally, Ornus will send inspiration to his faithful as a way to guide them in their creations on the path of what he deems perfection.

**Rivalries**
Ornus holds rivalry with all deities associated with Chaos, as would any other deity of Law. Though he holds special enmity towards [Alpha, the First Warforged](alpha.md). Ornus believes Alpha to be an upstart deity who dares to lay claim on constructs, even if he was their creator. Ornus holds that all constructs, regardless of origin, fall within his domain and that they are all his. He views Alpha's claim as weak and based upon emotion, rather than on logic. The faithful of Alpha are not openly hostile towards Modrons or the faithful of Ornus, but they are not hospitable either. The same is true for the faithful of Ornus. With Modrons and Warforged being the only Living Constructs known to exist, they both hold that the method behind the creation of the other is heretical and antithetical to their entire existence. The Modrons hold that the spark of life given to them by their Primus is the only true way to create a living construct, and that the elemental spirit bound to the body of Warforged is a type of torture for it. They believe this existential crisis of their spirits is what led to the continued suffering of the Warforged people.

**Home Sphere**
Ornus originated on Mechanus as the Primus. As such, he does not hail from any sphere.
