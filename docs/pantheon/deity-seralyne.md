# Overview

- **Symbol**: Burning sun framed by silver wings
- **Home Plane**: Radiance
- **Alignment**: Lawful Good
- **Portfolio**: Radiance, Zeal, Crusades, Purity, Sunlight
- **Worshipers**: Paladins, clerics, rangers, inquisitors
- **Cleric Alignments**: LG, NG, LN
- **Domains**: Good, Law, Sun, Glory, Purification
- **Favored Weapon**: Scimitar

**Origins**
Seralyne was born in the Floating City of Sewich, the beating heart of the adventuring orders that watch over Antæra. Even as a child, she shone with an inner light that never dimmed. As she grew into her Aasimar heritage, it became clear she was more than gifted—she was chosen. She rose quickly among the sun-paladins, her devotion to the light becoming legend. When word of undead stirring in the Angheh region reached Sewich, she led a crusade to cleanse it. The battle that followed changed everything. In a final act of defiance, she unleashed a divine blaze that scorched the land into the Angheh Wastes. In that instant, her soul ascended and she became a Solar—but her army was lost, and the undead claimed the land. That failure never left her. It became the fire that still drives her.

**Description**
Seralyne appears as a towering woman in golden armor, her hair and eyes glowing like fire held still. Silver-flame wings rise behind her, and when she enters a place, the air tightens like the moment before dawn. Her presence carries the intensity of the sun—steady, searing, inescapable. She doesn't raise her voice. She doesn't have to. Just being near her is enough to burn away doubt. Her clergy are unmistakable: white and gold robes, bright steel, and eyes that never look away from shadow. They are known as zealots in many corners of the world—and they do not deny it. Every member takes a sacred oath upon joining, binding themselves to the cause with fire and word. To stray from that path is to break faith with the light itself.

**Dogma**
Darkness breeds in silence. If you do nothing, it grows. You don’t wait for evil to act—you strike first. Destroy the undead. Banish what crawls in the dark. Cleanse what’s been touched by corruption. Bring light to places where light has never been. Be relentless. Be blinding. You are the edge of the dawn, and you do not break. Redemption comes through fire, and loyalty is proven in oath and deed.

**Home Sphere**
Seralyne’s light rises from the Antæran Sphere, a place of harmony and rhythm where seasons, stars, and magic all move in step. She was born on Antæra itself, in the skyborne city of Sewich—home to the world’s adventurers and guardians. Life flourishes across its lands, from oceans to forests, deserts to snowfields. But not all wounds heal. Her ascension carved the Angheh Wastes into the world—a scorched, haunted place where her army fell and her enemy endured. Now she watches from the Plane of Radiance, but her heart never left the skies of her home.
