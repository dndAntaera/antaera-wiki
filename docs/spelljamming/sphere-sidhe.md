# The Sidhe Sphere

The Sidhe Sphere feels less like a corner of the cosmos and more like something whispered into being. Wildspace here doesn’t stretch into emptiness—it sways, shimmers, glows. The void is full of drifting auroras, strands of golden pollen, and lights that flit about like shy spirits. Breathing here feels like drinking starlight. Time doesn’t pass—it meanders, circling back on itself like a song you almost remember. Spelljammers speak in hushed, wistful tones about their time here, often trailing off mid-sentence, as if part of them never really came back.

At the center of it all is Solaniel, a sun so gentle and luminous it feels more like a memory than a star. It doesn't burn—it hums, pulses, glows with the kind of light that makes you want to close your eyes and just listen. The fey believe it’s alive, and not just alive, but watching. They say it remembers every face it touches, every song sung under its light. Sometimes, when you’re alone under its warmth, you’ll hear laughter, or a lullaby, or the creak of a garden gate you’ve never seen before. Solaniel doesn’t pull things into orbit—it invites them.

The planets around it feel more like old dreams than celestial bodies. Each one has its own rhythm, its own story still unfolding. Solivagus wanders beneath twin moons, its caravans guided by wind and ritual. Silvaevera hums with blooming forests and shifting auroras, always just on the edge of the Feywild. Even the cold outer worlds—Glaciombra, Taedaflora—feel like faded lullabies, quiet and full of meaning you can’t quite name. Travelers often stop just to breathe the air, to walk the hills, to feel something they didn’t know they were missing. And when it’s time to leave, they don’t. Or they try—and hesitate.

Then there's the Bloomwound Oracle, always floating, always blooming just out of reach. They say it hears your heart before your mouth can speak. Druids tend it like a sacred grove, not with tools or rules, but with silence, song, and presence. It shows you the life you might’ve lived if you’d turned left instead of right, if you’d said yes, if you’d stayed. People don’t go there for answers. They go to remember the question.

And drifting beyond it all, wrapped in silence too deep for breath, is the Tan’dur Anomaly. It doesn’t belong here. That’s the first thing anyone notices. Where everything else sings, it absorbs. Where everything glows, it dims. Made of tan starmetal and angles that make your head ache, it waits, humming in a voice you only hear in dreams. Some who pass too close weep for reasons they can’t explain. Some forget why they came. And some... just stop. Staring. Listening.

# Planetology & Astrology

- Type of Sphere: Standard
- Primary Body:
    - Name: Solaniel
    - Type: Fire Body
- Spelljamming Aware: No

## Inner Track (<200 million miles)

- **Planet Name**: Solivagus
    - **Distance**: 38 million miles
    - **Size Category**: C
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: A scorched and shifting desert world where ochre sands stretch to the horizon and winds carry the scent of forgotten rites. Nomadic elves and eladrin travel in slow-moving, bark-grown caravans, their settlements shaped as much by tradition as by need. They follow ancestral star-paths passed down in song. The twin moons—Jurenna and Vekh—appear and vanish in rhythmic cycles, marking sacred passages of frost and shadow when the veil between worlds grows thin.

- **Planet Name**: Altanova
    - **Distance**: 122 million miles
    - **Size Category**: D
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: Altanova is a realm of towering mesas and verdant canyons, its forests faintly aglow with ley-fed light. The trees are ancient, some turned to living stone, their roots tangled with lingering Feywild enchantments. Arboreal elves dwell high above, moving along the fossilized boughs of their ancestors. It is a land rich with quiet magic and deeper memory.

## Goldilocks Zone (200–300 million miles)

- **Planet Name**: Silvaevera
    - **Distance**: 176 million miles
    - **Size Category**: D
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: Gentle hills and vibrant coastlines define this fey-touched world. The land is known for its shifting trails and radiant meadows, where reality often folds in on itself. The moon Tiryn ebbs in tandem with planar tides, influencing the fabric of space and time. Many who travel here find paths altered or destinations transformed, as if the land itself is dreaming.

- **Planet Name**: Lauriflorens
    - **Distance**: 254 million miles
    - **Size Category**: E
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: Among alpine ridges crowned with crystalline laurel groves, stargazers tend to ancient observatories etched into the mountain stone. The skies are veiled by a permanent auroral ring, casting a quiet twilight across the peaks. The eladrin here are solemn and reclusive, guardians of old celestial lore and the silent patterns that move among the stars.

## Outer Track (>300 million miles)

- **Planet Name**: Frigessylva
    - **Distance**: 332 million miles
    - **Size Category**: F
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: This boreal expanse is a land of stillness and cold, where black pines rise like watchtowers beneath a slow-turning sky. Eladrin hunters pass silently through moonlit glades, using mirrored stones to disorient prey. The three moons above reflect haunting echoes of the forest, their overlapping light casting strange patterns across the snow-covered land.

- **Planet Name**: Glaciombra
    - **Distance**: 418 million miles
    - **Size Category**: E
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: A frozen world blanketed in silence and shadow, Glaciombra is host to vast fungal networks of eerie intelligence. Drow communities thrive in deep caverns beneath the ice, among glowing lichen and sentient molds. Near the pole lies a shattered Seelie citadel, its ruined halls sealed in glacial silence. The dreams that echo there are not always one’s own.

- **Planet Name**: Taedaflora
    - **Distance**: 480 million miles
    - **Size Category**: H
    - **Planet Type**: Earth type
    - **Shape**: Spherical
    - **Other Notes**: A bleak and frostbound world, Taedaflora is a realm of endless taiga forests, locked in ice and pale light. Antlered eladrin traverse the wastes in great sleigh-fortresses drawn by silent beasts, their paths charted more by memory than map. The twin moons—Iskar and Velrien—circle with solemn grace, their presence guiding dreamwalkers through the echoes of ancient seasons.
