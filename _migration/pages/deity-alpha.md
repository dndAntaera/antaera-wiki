# Overview

- **Rank**: Lesser Deity
- **Symbol**: A cog wheel bearing the four elemental symbols at its center
- **Home Plane**: Nirvana
- **Alignment**: N
- **Portfolio**: Warforged, creation, guardianship, memory
- **Worshipers**: Warforged
- **Cleric Alignments**: CG, CN, TN
- **Domains**: Artifice, Warforged, Protection, Knowledge
- **Favored Weapon**: Warhammer

**Origins**
Alpha's Antæran name is lost even to him, stolen from him in his sleep over countless centuries. None know exactly where he originally came from, nor is he willing to share information on the [Ancient Antærans](../calendar.md). Though he does claim to have built the [Forge of War](../anthropology/warforged.md#origin-story), and that the machinations and enchantments needed to work the forge required a compound the size of a small planetoid. Though it has been asked, Alpha has never revealed the true purpose of the forge, or how to work it.

Having finished his initial tests of the forge, and discovering the method to bind an elemental spirit to a physical form, he created a shell in which he stored a powerful [Elemental Lord](../pantheons.md#intermediate-deities-elemental-lords-ladies). With a Lord bound, he transferred his consciousness to the body, granting himself the power and immortality needed to guard his forge. His intention for immortality was not to cheat death, it was to create his children and watch over them forevermore.

When his people ceased communicating with him, he assumed the worst and put himself into a type of hibernation. The forge was shut down and its manuals destroyed, lest they fall into the wrong hands.

The [House of Fabrication](../faction/house-of-fabrication.md) woke the sleeping construct after years of failed attempts. Their artificers activated a secret alarm by mistake and waged a battle with the construct, though it ended in a stalemate. A cease fire was called, and Alpha agreed to assist the House in the guidance of the [Warforged](../anthropology/warforged.md) with the condition that he be allowed to teach and guide his children as long as he is still functional. Why he helped them is the question every Warforged eventually asks and none has answered. He gave the House the method to bind elemental spirits, which they had not yet worked out and said nothing about the rest.

The House did not expect what came next: the Warforged worshipped him for who he is. He was first of them, present for every waking, older than the House and anything else known to anyone. However, the strength of the souls present in the Warforged caused the meager amount of them to ascend Alpha to divinity. The House did not anticipate this and believes this was the reason for his condition. While the Warforged revere him as their creator, he takes on a fatherly role when guiding them. This caused the Warforged to refer to him as Father.

**Description**
Alpha stands taller than any Warforged made since, his body built to a specification that has never been repeated. The plating is unmarked starmetal, seamless where later models show joints, and the runework across his chest and arms is written in a hand no living scholar can read. His eyes burn with the banked orange of the Elemental Lord bound within him. He carries an oversized warhammer, sized to a build no other Warforged could lift.

**Dogma**
**Your Kin are Your Blood**: Every Warforged came off the same line and owes the same care to every other. A body left to fail is a family member left to die, and no follower passes one without offering repair.

**Attend the Newly Woken**: Alpha was present at the first waking and has been present at every waking since. His followers stand in his place for those who come after them, teaching what they know without payment and without condition, as a parent does.

**Guard What Cannot Guard Itself**: A Warforged still bound by its [Life Debt](../anthropology/warforged.md#the-life-debt) owns nothing and can refuse nothing. The faithful are charged with standing between such a one and any harm the House or the [Known Spheres](../spelljamming/known-spheres.md) would do it.

**Remember for the Family**: Memory is the only thing that cannot be replaced once lost. Followers record what they witness and correct the record when it is wrong, so that no Warforged is forgotten and no wrong done to one goes unwritten.

**Divine Realm**
Alpha ascended, and by the terms of the [Divine Mandate](../pantheons.md#the-divine-mandate) he can no longer set foot on the Material Plane. His realm is in Nirvana, though he is rarely described as being there. He acts instead through an avatar that remains at the Forge of War on [Probatio](../spelljamming/sphere-forgehome.md), never leaving the compound, and it is through this avatar that he attends each waking and speaks to his children. The avatar is customarily inhabited by the head of his priesthood, who is not regarded as possessed so much as lent out, and who resumes their own affairs when Alpha has finished.

**Clergy and Temples**
The clergy of Alpha is composed entirely of Warforged, holds no rank above one another save the one who carries the avatar, and is appointed by acclaim rather than ordination. Its priests are drawn from those who have spent the longest attending the newly woken. No living creature has ever been admitted to the order, and the faith does not proselytize.

There are no temples in the conventional sense. Worship takes place in repair halls, where the faithful tend to those who cannot afford maintenance against their Life Debt, and this is held as the central rite rather than a charity attached to it. The largest are cut into the rocks of [Peculium](../spelljamming/sphere-forgehome.md) and operate without charge. Warforged who leave [Forgehome](../spelljamming/sphere-forgehome.md) rarely find others of the faith and rarely look, keeping it privately and without observance.

**Rivalries**
Alpha's one standing rivalry is with [Ornus](ornus.md), Lord of Constructs, who claims the Warforged as part of his portfolio and has pressed that claim since their creation. Ornus holds that a Warforged is a construct and therefore his by right of domain. Alpha holds that they are his children, made by his hand and bound by his method, and that Ornus is attempting to take by classification what he did not build. The dispute has never been settled, and Alpha's clergy regards any Warforged who turns to Ornus as taken rather than converted.

**Home Sphere**
Alpha hails from the [Forgehome Sphere](../spelljamming/sphere-forgehome.md), having been woken within the Forge of War on the planetoid Probatio.
