# Background Information
A background must be selected at 1st level and a character may only select one. Additionally, you are considered to have max ranks in the chosen associated skill that is not the associated profession skill.

The benefits of some of the presented backgrounds are only usable where applicable, or where they make sense to use. For example, an artisan cannot simply reduce the cost of an item, he must find someone who recognizes his abilities and is friendly towards him who then gives him a discount when purchasing the materials. When referenced, the associated skill is the skill in the “Associated Skill” entry that is not the listed profession skill.

# General Backgrounds

If a character has 5 ranks in the associated profession skill, they gain a +2 insight bonus on Diplomacy checks to alter the attitude of an NPC with ranks in the associated profession as well as a +2 synergy bonus on the chosen associated skill that is not the associated profession.

### Artisan
*You are an unaffiliated artisan, a craftsman without a guild or master. While your skills are not officially recognized, those you encounter see it for what it is worth.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (artisan), plus any one of the following: Appraise, Craft (any, chosen when taken), Knowledge (architecture & engineering)
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per month, you may reduce the gold cost of a single item you craft by 5%.

### Charlatan
*You use your natural wiles to confuse and deceive friend and foe alike. Those you encounter are usually none the wiser.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (charlatan), plus any one of the following: Bluff, Gather Information, Sense Motive
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per day when rolling a Bluff, Gather Information, or Sense Motive check, you may reroll the check before the DM declares whether the roll results in success or failure. You must take the result of the reroll, even if it’s worse than the original roll.

### Criminal
*You have lived a life of crime, and have become relatively proficient at it. Those you encounter are none the wiser.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (criminal), plus any one of the following: Bluff, Forgery, Sleight of Hand
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +2 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected.

### Diplomat
*You have attended court for a considerable amount of time, and therefore are familiar with the intricacies of politics and diplomatic situations.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (courtier), plus any one of the following: Diplomacy, Knowledge (nobility & royalty), Sense Motive
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per day, as a swift action, you may take 10 on Diplomacy checks, even when distractions normally would not allow it.

### Disciple
*Your deity or patron has reached out to you, and you haven answered with your unwavering devotion. Whether it be divine or profane, you heed the call of something greater than yourself.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (disciple), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (local), Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (religion)
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per month, you may pray to your patron or deity to gain the effect of an Augury spell as a supernatural ability at a caster level equal to your character level. Doing so requires no material component, though you must provide the focus.

### Entertainer
*You have spent your time honing your chosen performance and your talents are recognized by those you meet.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (entertainer), Perform (any)
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per week, when you attempt to make money with your performance skill, you may add a +2 insight bonus to the check.

### Merchant
*You either come from a long line of merchants or are a self-made entrepreneur. Either way, you know your way around the business world.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (merchant), plus any one of the following: Appraise, Diplomacy, Sense Motive
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per month, you may purchase a single item for 5% less than the asking price.

### Noble
*You were raised in high society and had a wealthy benefactor, or you were highborn in some kingdom and came from wealth.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (courtier), plus any one of the following: Craft (any, chosen when taken), Knowledge (local), Knowledge (nobility & royalty)
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: You begin the game with an extra 300 gp worth of equipment. This is not actual gold, but rather an allowance from which you can purchase additional items when making your character. Any gold leftover from this allowance after finishing your character is simply lost.

### Recluse
*For one reason or another, you were driven to seclusion. This isolation forced you to adapt to the harshness of the wilds.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (herbalist), plus any one of the following: Handle Animal, Knowledge (nature), Survival
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +2 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected.

### Sailor
*Whether it is a ship that sails the ocean, the sky, or the Astral Sea, you are skilled at manning one of these vessels.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (sailor), plus any one of the following: Balance, Use Rope, Tumble
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +2 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected.

#### Scholar
*As a wandering scholar and academic, you have traveled far and wide and collected a general knowledge of many things.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (scholar), plus Spellcraft or Knowledge (any, chosen when taken)
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per day as a swift action, gain a +2 insight bonus to your next associated skill check. For every additional 5 ranks in the associated profession you possess, the bonus increases by an additional +2.

### Soldier
*You once served in a military unit or a band of mercenaries. Your experience has made you a cut above the rest.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (soldier), plus any one of the following: Balance, Ride, Tumble
**Type**: General
**Benefit**: Once per encounter, as a swift action, when you make a skill check with one of your associated skills that is not your associated profession, you may gain a +2 insight bonus on that skill check.

# Regional Backgrounds
A character with a regional background gains a +2 insight bonus on Diplomacy checks to adjust the attitude of an NPC that is also a native of the appropriate region. If a character has 5 ranks in the associated profession skill, they gain a +2 synergy bonus on the chosen associated skill that is not the associated profession.
### Ironpine Native
*You grew up in either the Greater or Lesser Ironpine Forest. As such, you have adapted to living in an unforgiving forested area.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to either the Greater or Lesser Ironpine Forest
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Climb, Handle Animal, Survival
**Type**: Regional
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +2 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected. Additionally, you gain Sylvan as an automatic language.

### Antæran Plains Native
*As a citizen of the New Haven Imperium, you grew up in a culture that is heavily militaristic and xenophobic.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Antæran Plains, Human
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (nobility & royalty), Martial Lore, Tumble
**Type**: Regional
**Benefit**: You gain the Martial Weapon Proficiency feat for any one martial weapon you choose as a bonus feat. This feat cannot be retrained.

### Desert Dweller
*You were raised on the sand and under the sun, your body has adapted to surviving the harshness of the desert.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to either the Kir’Raask Desert or the Angheh Wastes
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (geography), Survive, Tumble
**Type**:Regional
**Benefit**: You gain the Heat Endurance feat as a bonus feat. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain Ignan as an automatic language.

### Gulf Native
*You were raised in the heartland of the elves. As such, your elven heritage is more refined.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Mir’Raask Gulf, Elf
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Search, Spot, Listen
**Type**: Regional
**Benefit**: Your racial bonus on Search, Spot, and Listen is increased to +4.

### Islander
*Your people are the natives of the Broken Spire Isles. They are legendary sailors and at home on the sea as they are on the land.*

**Prerequisite**: Native of the Broken Spire Isles
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (nature), Use Rope
**Type**: Regional
**Benefit**: You gain the Sea Legs feat as a bonus feat. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Knowledge (nature) checks related to aquatic creatures or other maritime subjects.

### Mountain Dweller
*You grew up on the mountain top, surrounded by snow and at a higher elevation. You have adapted to this climate.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to a mountain range
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), choose any one of the following: Climb, Knowledge (geography), Survival
**Type**: Regional
**Benefit**: You gain Altitude Adaptation as a bonus feat. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus on Survival checks made to navigate mountain ranges.

### Sewich Adventurer
*You were, or your ancestor was, one of the original members of the Sewich Union, having hailed from an entirely different reality altogether. You and your people are of a unique culture of your own, and as such are highly adaptable.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Sea of Innas
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), choose any one of the following: Knowledge (local), Search, Survival
**Type**: Regional
**Benefit**: You gain +1 skill point at 1st level, and 1 additional skill point every level after.

# Guild Backgrounds
A character with a guild background gains a +2 insight bonus on Diplomacy checks to adjust the attitude of an NPC that is of the same guild. If a character has 5 ranks in the associated profession skill, they gain a +2 synergy bonus on the chosen associated skill that is not the associated profession.

Additionally, to maintain the benefits of these backgrounds, a character must pay tithe to their respective guild in an amount equal to 5 x ECL in gold each month or they lose the benefits of the background. Failing to pay the tithe causes your background to become the General equivalent of your Guild Background. If you are removed from your guild, your background becomes the General equivalent of it.

You must work with the DM to pick a guild to which you belong.
### Guild: Artisan
*You work for one of the great guilds as an artisan and as such gain certain benefits when dealing with your guild.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (artisan), plus any one of the following: Appraise, (any, chosen when taken), Knowledge (architecture & engineering)
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per month, you may reduce the gold cost of a single item you craft by 10%.

### Guild: Charlatan
*Your guild employs your wiles to infiltrate competitors and gain information from them.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (charlatan), plus any one of the following: Bluff, Gather Information, Sense Motive
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per day when rolling a Bluff, Gather Information, or Sense Motive check, you may reroll the check before the DM declares whether the roll results in success or failure. You must take the result of the reroll, even if it’s worse than the original roll. Additionally, you gain a +5 insight bonus on the reroll.

### Guild: Criminal
*You belong to a criminal organization, doing various dirty deeds for your superiors.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (criminal), plus any one of the following: Bluff, Forgery, Sleight of Hand
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +4 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected.

### Guild: Diplomat
*The royal society you belong to has trained you in the intricacies of courtly conduct, to whom you also owe your standings.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (courtier), plus any one of the following: Diplomacy, Knowledge (nobility & royalty), Sense Motive
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per day, as a swift action, you may take 10 on Diplomacy checks, even when distractions normally would not allow it. Additionally, you gain a +2 insight bonus on reputation checks.

### Guild: Disciple
*You have joined the ranks of your clergy or cult. You proudly spread the ordeals of your faith as a devotee.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (disciple), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (local), Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (religion)
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per week, you may pray to your patron or deity to gain the effect of an Augury spell as a supernatural ability at a caster level equal to your character level. Doing so requires no material component, though you must provide the focus.

### Guild: Entertainer
*You have joined a college or troupe wherein you have learned to hone your skills as an entertainer.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (entertainer), Perform (any, chosen when taken)
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per week, when you attempt to make money with your performance skill, you may add a +4 insight bonus to the check. Additionally, you gain a +2 insight bonus on reputation checks.

### Guild: Merchant
*You are employed by a mercantile company, you trade in goods and make deals under the name of your employer.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (merchant), plus any one of the following: Appraise, Diplomacy, Sense Motive
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per month, you may purchase a single item for 10% less than the asking price.

### Guild: Sailor
*You belong to some sort of organization that runs one or more sailing ships. Whether it is a ship that sails the ocean, the sky, or the Astral Sea, you are skilled at manning one of these vessels.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (sailor), plus any one of the following: Balance, Use Rope, Tumble
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +4 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected. Additionally, once per month, you may waive the fee for traveling on a sailing vessel.

### Guild: Scholar
*You attend an academic college or university to further your studies. Due to these studies, you have collected knowledge of many things.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (scholar), plus Spellcraft or Knowledge (any, chosen when taken)
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per day as a swift action, gain a +4 insight bonus to your next associated skill check. For every additional 5 ranks in the associated profession you possess, the bonus increases by an additional +2.

### Guild: Soldier
*You serve in a military unit or a band of mercenaries. Your experience has made you a cut above the rest.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (soldier), plus any one of the following: Balance, Ride, Tumble
**Type**: Guild
**Benefit**: Once per encounter, as a swift action, when you make a skill check with one of your associated skills that is not your associated profession, you may gain a +4 insight bonus on that skill check.

# Extraplanar Backgrounds
Extraplanar backgrounds have prerequisites, and may only be taken if your character is from that plane.

A character with an Extraplanar background gains the Extraplanar subtype when not on their native plane, if they didn’t already. If a character has 5 ranks in the associated profession skill, they gain a +2 synergy bonus on the chosen associated skill that is not the associated profession.
### Aqualorn Native
*You were born and raised in the Aqualorn Expanse, as such your ancestors hailed from the Elemental Plane of Water.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Aqualorn Expanse
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Water Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Survival checks when navigating large bodies of water.

### Chaotic Planar Native
*You were born and raised on a chaoticly aligned plane, as such your ancestors were likely either outsiders from such planes or had lived on the plane for an extended period of time.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to a plane with the Chaos planar trait
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Anarchic Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus on saving throws made to resist the Chaotic planar effects of planes.

### Earthspire Native
*You were born and raised in the Earthspire Expanse, as such your ancestors hailed from the Elemental Plane of Earth.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Earthspire Expanse
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Earth Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Survival checks when navigating underground.

### Evil Planar Native
*You were born and raised on an evil aligned plane, as such your ancestors were likely either outsiders from such planes or had lived on the plane for an extended period of time.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to a plane with the Evil planar trait
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Fiendish Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus on saving throws made to resist the Evil planar effects of planes.

### Good Planar Native
*You were born and raised on a good aligned plane, as such your ancestors were likely either outsiders from such planes or had lived on the plane for an extended period of time.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to a plane with the Celestial planar trait
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Celestial Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus on saving throws made to resist the Good planar effects of planes.

### Lawful Planar Native
*You were born and raised on a lawfully aligned plane, as such your ancestors were likely either outsiders from such planes or had lived on the plane for an extended period of time.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to a plane with the Law planar trait
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Axiomatic Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus on saving throws made to resist the Lawful planar effects of planes.

### Shadowlands Native
*You were born and raised in the Shadowlands, as such your ancestors were undead or other shadowy creatures.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Shadowlands
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Shadow Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Survival checks when navigating at night or in non-magical darkness.

### Sidhe Native
*You were born and raised in the Sidhe Wilderness, as such your ancestors are fey or other fey-like creatures.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Sidhe Wilderness
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Fey Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Survival checks when navigating forests or other large forested areas.

### Tainted Native
*You were born and raised on a tainted plane or a place with an extreme concentration of taint; as such, your body has developed a stronger resistance to its effects, but at a cost.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to a tainted plane
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Intimidate
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the [Pure Soul](http://antaera.wikidot.com/the-index#:~:text=cost%20by%2025%25.-,Pure%20Soul,-%5BExalted%5D) feat for free at 1st level without needing to meet the prerequisites, nor do you lose the feat for changing alignment as per normal with Exalted feats. This feat cannot be retrained. If you would gain this feat from another source, you instead gain a +1 bonus to Depravity and Corruption checks as if you had an additional Exalted feat. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Intimidate checks. Additionally, you gain an Aura as a Cleric. The aura registers with the Law/Order axis of your alignment and as Evil. For example, a character that is Lawful Neutral has a Lawful Evil Aura.

### Zephyr Native
*You were born and raised in the Zephyr Haven, as such your ancestors hailed from the Elemental Plane of Air.*

**Prerequisite**: Native to the Zephyr Haven
**Associated Skills**: Profession (choose one), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (local), Survival
**Type**: Extraplanar
**Benefit**: You gain the Air Heritage feat for free at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained. You gain a +2 insight bonus to Survival checks when navigating at high altitudes, such as when flying or on an airship.

# Spelljamming Backgrounds
You are native to a spelljamming-aware society. As such, you gain the Spacefarer feat at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained.

### Astral Diplomat
*You have been trained in the ways of the word. Your skills with politics and diplomacy serve you well.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (courtier), any one of the following: Diplomacy, Knowledge (nobility & royalty), Sense Motive
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: Once per day, as a swift action, you may take 10 on your next Diplomacy check, even when distractions normally would not allow it.

### Astral Engineer
*Through hands-on experience aboard spelljammers, you’ve become proficient in their maintenance, mastering the intricate mechanics and arcane systems that keep these magical vessels running smoothly.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (spacehand), plus any one of the following: Craft (shipwright - spelljammer), Knowledge (wildspace), Wildspace Survival
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: Once per month you may reduce the cost of building or repairing a spelljammer by 5%.

### Astral Entertainer
*Your troupe has spent considerable time among the stars, performing for any number of strange and mystical creatures of the realms.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (entertainer), plus any one of the following: Perform (any)
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: Once per week, when you attempt to make money with your performance skill, you may add a +2 insight bonus to the check.

### Astral Merchant
*You have sailed the vastness of wildspace, navigating between various crystal spheres and visiting the bustling markets scattered among them. Each filled with rare treasures and diverse cultures.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (merchant), plus any one of the following: Appraise, Diplomacy, Sense Motive
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: Once per month, you may buy or sell a single item for 5% less than the asking price.

### Astrologer
*As an academic among the stars, you have dedicated yourself to the study of wildspace and the mysteries beyond, unraveling the arcane forces and celestial phenomena that govern the cosmos.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (scholar), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (planetology), Knowledge (wildspace), Wildspace Survival
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: Once per day, you may gain a +2 insight bonus on the associated skill you selected. This bonus increases by 1 for every 5 ranks in the associated profession you possess.

**Associated Skills**: Profession (siege engineer), plus any one of the following: Craft (ship
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: You gain a +2 insight bonus to attack rolls made with shipbound siege weapons. This bonus increases by 1 for every 5 ranks in your associated profession skill.

### Shipboard Chaplain
*Chosen by your faith, you have taken up the sacred mission to carry its teachings to the farthest reaches of wildspace, spreading your beliefs to distant worlds and guiding lost souls.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (disciple), plus any one of the following: Knowledge (religion), Knowledge (the planes), Knowledge (wildspace)
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Benefit**: Once per day, while preparing your divine spells for the day, you may attempt a Knowledge (religion) check (DC 25 - your divine caster level) to attempt to make contact with your deity and prepare your spells for the day. You may only use this ability when your connection to your deity is cut off as a result of being in a crystal sphere where they are not worshiped.

*After years of rigorous training at a renowned spelljamming academy, you’ve mastered the arcane art of manning spelljammers, allowing you to navigate the vast, uncharted reaches of the cosmos.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (spacehand), plus any one of the following: Balance, Use Rope, Wildspace Survival

**Associated Skills**: Profession (wildspace navigator), plus any one of the following: Helm Pilot, Knowledge (wildspace), Wildspace Survival
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Prerequisite**: No caster or manifester level
**Benefit**: You gain the Apprentice Helmsman feat as a bonus feat at 1st level. This feat cannot be retrained.

### Wildspace Navigator, Talented
*You enrolled in one of the renowned spelljamming academies, where your natural talent for piloting quickly set you apart from your peers. With an instinctive connection to the helm and the stars themselves, your abilities have effortlessly guided you toward the vast reaches of the cosmos.*

**Associated Skills**: Profession (wildspace navigator), plus any one of the following: Helm Pilot, Knowledge (wildspace), Wildspace Survival
**Type**: Spelljamming
**Prerequisite**: Caster level 1st or Manifester level 1st, does not possess the Apprentice Helmsman feat
**Benefit**: Once per week, as an immediate action, you may take 10 on your next skill check with the associated skill you chose, even when distractions normally would not allow it.
