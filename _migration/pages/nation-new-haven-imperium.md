# Overview

The New Haven Imperium, known colloquially as the Imperium, is a human empire that rules the greater part of the eastern continent of Antæra from its capital at [New Haven](../settlement/imperial-capital-of-new-haven.md) on the [Antæran Plains](../region/antaeran-plains.md). It is governed by the Imperial family under the emperor Nicolaus Poplicola IX and holds that humanity is the apex of the mortal races, a doctrine it carries into every land it takes. The Imperium is militaristic, slaveholding, and xenophobic, and the humans of the [Known Spheres](../spelljamming/known-spheres.md) who live outside its borders regard it as the chapter of their history they would rather be rid of. [Imperial](../languages.md#imperial) is its language, and the inhabitants of every territory it conquers are made to learn it.

The Imperium is no longer confined to one world. The colony fleet [Ukrol](../deity/ukrol.md) took into wildspace conquered a crystal sphere outside the Known Spheres, [Concordia](../spelljamming/sphere-concordia.md), which the Imperium now claims as its capital, and reports from turncoats and captured soldiers describe the same tyranny and slavery on those worlds as on the Plains. Its deity Ukrol has issued a divine mandate for conquest, and the Imperium's attack on a [Gallamarketh](../spelljamming/sphere-gallamarketh.md) colony ship opened a holy war between it and the Known Spheres at large. Among the charges laid against it, the Imperium has broken the prohibition on gunpowder [firearms](../item/firearms.md) and is actively developing them.

# Origin Story

The Imperium began as a colony. Ukrol, a Netherese archmage of Realmspace, was sent into the stars to found a colony in the name of the Netherese Empire, and the humans aboard his vessel were a people the Netherese had bred for strength, intellect, and ambition. They entered the Antæran Cluster and attempted to settle among the populations of the spheres they reached, and were driven off each world in turn. They came at last to the [Antæran Sphere](../spelljamming/sphere-antaera.md), which had no spelljamming and no defense against it, and landed on the Antæran Plains.

The plan was to carve out a kingdom and build its population. The kingdom of New Haven was established on the Plains, and once it was secure Ukrol turned to the conquest of the rest of the continent. This was the [Era of Man](../calendar.md#3e-the-era-of-man). The empire spread for a thousand years, subjugating or exterminating the races in its path, and the elves, halflings, and dragons who resisted were driven to alliance against it. It ended when the Fist of Iron, a knightly order founded by [Leonus Ironmane](../pantheon/mortal-leonis.md), united warriors from across the continent and broke the human empire, forcing it back to the Plains. The Cult of Ukrol was dismantled and fell dormant. Ukrol himself, who had ascended to divinity during the long war on the worship it bred him, withdrew to his realm in Gehenna and took a secret colony fleet into wildspace with him.

What remained on the Plains was the kingdom of New Haven. It kept the practices of the old empire as culture rather than scripture, and endured the two thousand years of the [Long Winter](../calendar.md#4e-the-era-of-the-long-winter-frost-marches) behind its walls. When the ice retreated, the colony Ukrol had taken into the stars made contact with the kingdom. In the centuries since its departure the colony had conquered an entire crystal sphere beyond the Known Spheres, and it claimed that sphere as the new capital of the Imperium. The kingdom on Antæra found itself beholden to the colony, as the colony's military might was overwhelming. The transfer was not ill met, as submission to the stronger is part of the Imperium's own doctrine. The Imperium is careful not to expose the wider world of Antæra to spelljamming. The Antæran Constellation is under the protective status of the Known Spheres, and the Imperium does not intend to be accused of uplifting a sphere so heavily safeguarded against it. The Cult of Ukrol is believed to have been resurrected at the start of this era, and the Imperium's return to expansion dates from the same years.

# Social Order

The Imperium is ruled with an iron fist, and it holds that those too weak to lead are of better use serving. Society is arranged by contribution. Every citizen is entitled to a standard share of the Imperium's wealth, and those who give more to it receive more in return. Those who enlist in the legions are rewarded above those who are conscripted, and both stand above the enslaved. The nobility is made up of the Imperial family, the officers of the military, and the wealthiest Imperial merchants. Most noble houses have held their rank for generations, though a citizen may rise into the nobility through merit, and the military is the common road.

Non-humans are not outlawed in the Imperium, but they are not citizens in any sense that matters. In New Haven they are confined to the outcast quarter of [Stone Hook](../settlement/imperial-capital-of-new-haven.md#stone-hook) or admitted only as laborers and merchants under watch, and they are barred from the [Citadel](../settlement/imperial-capital-of-new-haven.md#the-citadel) outright by a barrier that repels any who are not human. The few who live among the nobility are slaves, and they are required to wear magical disguises so that their appearance does not offend their owners.

# Slavery

Slavery in the Imperium began in [Athelney](../settlement/athelney.md), the town nearest the capital, where the ducal house of Blademoore enslaved prisoners of war and those the Imperial family deemed deplorable. When the town's slaves raised its surrounding wall, the Imperium legalized and taxed the practice across all of its territories, and Athelney has remained the hub of the trade since. The Imperium does not discriminate in whom it enslaves; all prisoners of war are taken, and human slaves are treated only marginally better than the rest. In the current age the majority of slaves are goblinoids and kobolds, with political prisoners and prisoners of war making up the remainder.

A slave in the Imperium is freed only when their owner dies with no next of kin. The sanctioned markets, of which New Haven's is the most esteemed on the continent, do not sell humans on pain of losing their license. Humans are sold regardless, in the unsanctioned trade of Stone Hook, which the city does not acknowledge.

# Life in Concordia

The Imperium presents Concordia, its capital sphere, as the proof of its doctrine. Every citizen is housed, fed, schooled, and employed. None of the citizenry is poor, because the idle are conscripted, and criminality is almost nonexistent, because the convicted are enslaved and cease to be citizens. The cities are laid out in a grid design to instill an orderly and disciplined mindset in their citizens. Their walls and homes were built by the very slaves whose homes were demolished to build them. The Imperium holds that Concordia is what every sphere would be under competent rule, and it says so to every envoy it receives.

# The Imperial Legion

The Imperium's military is considered one of the most skilled and brutal on the continent. The Imperatoria Militaris is officered by graduates of the New Haven College of War, the most prestigious military academy in the land, which draws students from across Antæra. Humans are nearly always admitted; anyone else requires a referral. A student on a military scholarship owes the Imperium four years of service on graduation, and most who graduate either take a commission or leave to become adventurers.

Outside the capital, the Imperium has replaced most of the militaries of its controlled territories with soldier slaves. New Haven itself is the exception and is garrisoned by citizens. The Imperium claims the [Aesc Wood](../region/aesc-wood.md), where a manifest zone is said to open onto the [Feywild](../plane/feywild.md), and has attempted to seize it from the [Sylvan Sect](../faction/sylvan-sect.md) several times for the tactical advantage it would confer. It has not succeeded. [Aberystwyth](../settlement/aberystwyth.md), once an Imperial fortress built to defend against siege by sea, declared independence and is now a neutral prison island that holds criminals for every nation on the continent, the Imperium included.

# Firearms

The Imperium manufactures gunpowder firearms in open defiance of the prohibition observed across the Known Spheres. The powder works are kept on Officina, the manufacturing world of Concordia, far enough from the capital that an accident there reaches nothing the Imperium values. The Imperium considers the prohibition a weakness of the spheres that keep it, and the firearms it fields are among the charges the Known Spheres lay against it.

# Faith

In the kingdom on Antæra, state worship is mandated to Superbia, who holds the civic calendar and the rites every citizen attends. The religious center of the kingdom is within the Citadel at New Haven, and it is believed to be dedicated to Superbia as well, though it is rumored to belong to Ukrol. The barrier around the Citadel that repels non-humans is held by scholars outside the Imperium to be a misuse of a Mythal, and the question is a standing subject of international dispute. Those who accuse the Imperium of it openly tend to die of natural causes within a few weeks.

Across the stars, the Imperium worships Ukrol openly. The Imperium holds that Ukrol guides it in its conquests. He does not guide every conquest, but he blesses those that are carried out in his name, and a campaign that is begun with his rites is held to carry his favor. The Imperium's gods together form the [Imperial Pantheon](../pantheons.md#the-imperial-pantheon), whose members ascended from the Imperium's own leaders and folk heroes through the weight of human worship. The pantheon is worshipped solely by humans, and a non-human who takes up its worship receives nothing from it. It is commonly held across the continent that the Imperium is the remnant of the Cult of Ukrol.

# Diplomacy

The Imperium regards the Known Spheres' refusal to recognize Concordia as an insult and intends to correct it. It does not permit outsiders to settle in the sphere. Those admitted on diplomatic passage are escorted from the shell to the capital and back along a single approved course, and are shown a scripted facade designed to present the sphere as a peaceful and prosperous place, and not the imperial dictatorship that it is.

# The Holy War

A Gallamarketh colony ship, sent out to settle a derelict sphere to relieve the overpopulation of the Known Spheres, entered Imperial territory without leave. It was hailed and failed to identify either its destination or its intent, and Imperial spelljammers fired on it as Imperial law requires of any vessel that does so. Its survivors crash-landed in an unknown derelict sphere. The Known Spheres answered with a declaration of war, and in the course of it discovered the Imperium's capital sphere beyond their borders.

The Imperium holds the war to be a holy one. Ukrol has issued a divine mandate for conquest, and the Imperium regards the Known Spheres' declaration as the opening it was waiting for, since a war begun by the enemy is a war it may prosecute without restraint. Gallamarketh has answered by hiring adventurers and mercenaries for the defense of the spheres, offering a stipend and a low-end spelljammer to any new party that signs on. The Imperium considers these hirelings pirates and treats them as such.
