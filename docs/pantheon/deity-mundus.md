---
title: "Mundus, Goddess of Life"
---

- **Symbol**: Oak tree
- **Home Plane**: Elysium
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
- **Portfolio**: Life, Nature, Light
- **Worshipers**: Cleric, druid, healer, farmer, ranger, midwife
- **Cleric Alignments**: LG, NG, CG, N
- **Domains**: Life, Healing, Sun, Nature, Good
- **Favored Weapon**: Quarterstaff

**Origins**
Mundus was created by the will of reality itself. She was not born nor raised to godhood, but became as a necessity—formed in the earliest breath of creation to tend the first roots and cradle the spark of life.

**Description**
Mundus never manifests directly. Instead, she reveals herself through the flourishing of life: an infant’s cry, a blooming tree, the return of warmth after winter. She is often called Mother Nature, and those who follow her see her hand in every nurturing act. Her rites are practiced in groves, at springs, in birth houses, and fields at planting time.

**Dogma**
All life is sacred. Nurture what grows. Heal the sick, protect the weak, and never take more than is needed. Let each generation pass on the world in better shape than they found it. Where life falters, restore it; where it blooms, rejoice.

**Home Sphere**
Mundus has no known native crystal sphere or home planet. She is a primal constant found anywhere life rises from earth or breath.
